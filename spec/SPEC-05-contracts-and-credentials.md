## 1. Relationship Between Grants and Contracts

In SealLink v1.0, a **unilateral grant (`SPEC-06`) is the base authorization primitive**: it requires no recipient assent and imposes no obligations on the recipient. A **contract (`seallink-contract/1`)** is the bilateral or multilateral overlay that adds mutual agreement, obligations, framework governance, and shared or arbiter-controlled termination (`SL-CONT-001`, `decided`).

- A contract MAY exist without any grant (e.g., an anchored agreement between two people or organizations).
- Any action profile imposing payment, work, rental, or liability obligations on the recipient (such as `rental` or contract-bound `agent/session`) MUST declare `requires_contract = true`; executors refuse unilateral grants for such actions (`GRANT_CONTRACT_REQUIRED`).

## 2. N-Party Structured Contracts (`seallink-contract/1`)

### 2.1 Structure, Salted Selective Disclosure, and Machine-Clause Completeness (`SL-CONT-002`, `decided`)

A `seallink-contract/1` object (`<= 65536` bytes, `schemas/contract.schema.json`) binds `N >= 2` parties (any combination of people, organizations, agents, or unverified actors):

1. **Header fields & party binding:** `type`, `network`, `genesis`, `profile`, `contract_salt` (32 CSPRNG bytes, preventing offline guessing of short contracts), `parties[2..32]` — where each entry carries a mandatory unique-within-contract **`party_id`** (`^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9._-]{0,63}$`, allowing multiple parties to share a legal `role` such as co-tenants `"tenant"` with `"tenant-1"` and `"tenant-2"`; duplicate `party_id` refuses `WIRE_MEMBER`) and is either an **autonomous party** `{party_id, role, name_pin?, signing_policy: ThresholdPolicy}` or a **ward party** `{party_id, role, ward_origin_id, name_pin}` (omitting static `signing_policy` so guardian succession never invalidates a live contract) — `window` (`not_before_height`, optional `not_after_height`, optional calendar `not_after_unix`), optional `machine_clause_indices`, optional `framework_id`, optional/required `seal`, optional `assent_output`, optional `config_sha256`, and optional `moderation`.
   - **Autonomous `name_pin` & ward detection:** Whenever an autonomous party declares `name_pin`, the verifier MUST verify `(name, registration, owner_policy)` on the active chain, verify `party.signing_policy` matches that name's `link.grant` policy at the contract anchor height (or current tip pre-anchor), AND perform history-based origin detection (`SL-ORIG-003`): if that registration belongs to a non-emancipated ward, refusing `WARD_AUTONOMOUS_FORBIDDEN` prevents a ward from bypassing guardianship by listing itself as an autonomous party.
   - **Distinct party identities:** All entries in `contract.parties[]` MUST represent pairwise-distinct party identities (comparing `(name, registration, canonical owner_policy)` when `name_pin` is present, or `ward_origin_id`, or canonical `signing_policy` for key-only parties; duplicate party identities refuse `CONTRACT_TERMS_MISMATCH`).
   - **Grant participant binding:** For any grant bound to `contract_id`, BOTH the grant's `issuer` and its `recipient` MUST correspond to distinct parties listed in `contract.parties[]` (by `(name, registration, owner_policy)` for named actors, or exact `signing_policy` match for key/group recipients; `CONTRACT_TERMS_MISMATCH` otherwise).
2. **Clauses, Closed `ClauseObject` / `MachineClauseTerms`, & Selective Disclosure:** Each disclosed clause `i` conforms to the closed `ClauseObject` schema (`schemas/contract.schema.json`, `minProperties: 1`, `additionalProperties: false`): optional `title` (single-line string `1..256`), optional `text` (`1..16384`, where `\n` is allowed per `SL-WIRE-005`), and optional `machine_terms` (`MachineClauseTerms`, closed: required `capabilities[1..64]`, optional `config_sha256`, optional `not_before_height`, optional `not_after_height`). The contract stores ONLY the ordered array `clause_commitments[]` where:
   ```text
   clause_commitments[i] = H_tag("seallink-clause/1", salt_i || canonical_JCS(clause_i))
   contract_id           = SHA256(canonical_JCS(seallink-contract/1))
   ```
   When a grant binds `contract_id` (and optionally `framework_id`), both complete contract documents MUST be presented (`CONTRACT_NOT_PRESENTED`), MUST recompute to their bound identifiers (`CONTRACT_ID_MISMATCH`), and MUST NOT have persisted `contract` or `framework` closures in the local journal (`SEAL_SPENT`).
3. **Deterministic machine-clause completeness & narrowing:** Every clause index carrying machine-enforced terms MUST be listed in strictly ascending order in `machine_clause_indices[]` (with every index `< len(clause_commitments)`). An executor MUST require disclosure of `(salt_i, clause_i)` for EVERY index `i` in `machine_clause_indices[]`, verify `commit(salt_i, clause_i) == clause_commitments[i]`, validate `clause_i` against `ClauseObject` requiring `clause_i.machine_terms` to be present and valid under `MachineClauseTerms`, and mechanically verify that: (a) every capability in `grant.capabilities[]` narrows (`SL-WIRE-017`) at least one capability declared across the disclosed `machine_terms.capabilities[]`, (b) any declared `machine_terms.config_sha256` equals `contract.config_sha256`, and (c) the grant's block window nests inside any declared `machine_terms.not_before_height` / `machine_terms.not_after_height` (`CONTRACT_TERMS_MISMATCH` on missing disclosure or mismatch). Prose-only clauses outside `machine_clause_indices[]` MAY remain undisclosed to the executor.
4. **Framework Nesting & Window Enforcement (`SL-CONT-003`, `decided`):** A contract MAY name at most one `framework_id`, and a framework contract MUST NOT name another framework contract (maximum nesting depth = 1). Every grant's block window MUST nest inside its bound contract's window (`not_before_height` and optional `not_after_height`), which MUST nest inside its framework contract's window (`CONTRACT_TERMS_MISMATCH` on violation). Whenever `contract.window.not_after_unix` or `framework.window.not_after_unix` is present, verifiers MUST refuse `GRANT_WINDOW_CLOSED` once decision time `now >= not_after_unix`.

### 2.2 Symmetric Native Assent (`seallink-assent/1`) and Anchoring (`SL-CONT-004`, `decided`)

1. **Symmetric off-chain assent (`AssentPayload`):** Every `seallink-assent/1` (`<= 32768` bytes, accommodating multi-guardian `guardian_approvals[]` and up to 32-of-32 `SignatureSet`) carries closed members `(type, network, genesis, target_type, target_id, party_id, party_role, issued_at, expires_at, nonce)` plus optional `party_name` (REQUIRED when `target_type ∈ {"seallink-guardian/1", "seallink-migration/1"}`, and verified against `party.name_pin.name` when present on contracts) and optional `guardian_context` (REQUIRED for ward parties; paired strictly with `guardian_approvals[]`). A contract is concluded when EVERY distinct `party_id` in `parties[]` is covered 1-to-1 by a valid `seallink-assent/1` (`target_type = "seallink-contract/1"`, `target_id = contract_id`, matching `party_id` and `party_role == party.role`; duplicate or missing `party_id` assents refuse `CONTRACT_ASSENT_INVALID`):
   - Permitted signer roles for `seallink-assent/1` are strictly `grant` and `guardian_quorum` (`registry/object-types.json`, coherent with `registry/key-roles.json`); `auth` and `device` keys MUST NEVER sign `seallink-assent/1` (`WIRE_SIGNATURE`).
   - For an **autonomous party** (`signing_policy` present), `seallink-assent/1` uses the ordinary `{"payload","signatures"}` envelope whose `signatures.policy` MUST strictly equal `party.signing_policy` (`SL-WIRE-013`).
   - For a **ward party** (`ward_origin_id` present, `SPEC-04`), `seallink-assent/1` MUST carry `payload.guardian_context` (`origin_id == party.ward_origin_id`, `ward_name == party.name_pin.name`, `event_class = "contract_assent"`, `revision` matching that ward's active roster revision) and top-level `guardian_approvals[]` satisfying `required("contract_assent")` across distinct active guardian slots (`SL-ORIG-006` refusal precedence: `WARD_AUTONOMOUS_FORBIDDEN`, then `ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED`, then `GUARDIAN_INACTIVE`, then `WIRE_SIGNATURE`, then `GUARDIAN_QUORUM_UNMET`). Contracts MAY include **multiple distinct ward parties**; verifiers MUST resolve each ward party against its own origin bundle keyed by `party.ward_origin_id` (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` if any ward party lacks a verified matching origin bundle).
   - **Historical ward representation at confirmed anchor height `h_anchor` & live `0x0b` parity:** Once a contract is anchored on chain at confirmed height `h_anchor` (via `0x07` or `0x01`), each ward party's `seallink-assent/1` — specifically its ward name resolution (`party_name` vs active binding at `h_anchor`), active guardian roster revision, active slot states, and each approving guardian's `link.grant` signing policy — MUST be verified **at `h_anchor`** (and checked against any retroactive `0x0b` `CredentialBreak` for `role = "grant"`: `h_anchor < h_c` survives, while `h_anchor >= h_c` refuses **`CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`**), rather than against tip. Likewise, live unanchored `verify_assent` (`anchor_height = None`) MUST unconditionally check `0x0b` `CredentialBreak` at `tip_height` for autonomous AND ward parties (`CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`). Consequently, subsequent `0x0f` roster revisions or guardian key rotations NEVER break already-concluded contracts, while contract window, unspent contract seal, and any *new* ward grants/invocations continue to be evaluated at tip.
   - **Assent freshness anchored at conclusion/issuance:** Assent freshness is evaluated strictly against the confirmed **`block_time`** of the `0x07` contract anchor (or confirmed `0x01` grant anchor for unanchored contract grants; missing anchor `block_time` MUST fail closed with `CHAIN_FRAME_INVALID` — NEVER silently falling back to local wall-clock `now`) using exact bounds:
     ```text
     issued_at <= anchor_time < expires_at   AND   expires_at - issued_at <= 259200   AND   anchor_time - issued_at <= 259200
     ```
     (`<= 72` hours; `CONTRACT_ASSENT_INVALID` on stale, expired, overlong, or post-dated assent at anchor time). Concluded contracts therefore remain valid throughout their declared `window` without re-testing assent freshness against later invocation wall-clock time.
2. **Contract anchor (`0x07`):** A contract is anchored via `OP_RETURN` action `0x07` committing `contract_id` (`loc_type = 0x00` default for private contracts).
3. **Mandatory on-chain ward assent anchoring & optional autonomous `assent_output` (`D14-H08`):** Because `0x07` commits only `contract_id`, for any anchored contract (`anchor_height` present) involving one or more **ward parties** (`ward_origin_id`), each ward party's `seallink-assent/1` MUST be cryptographically bound on chain at or before `h_anchor` via EITHER: (a) spending the contract's `N-of-N` `P2WSH` `assent_output` inside the confirmed `0x07` `contract_anchor_txid` transaction, OR (b) confirming an `OP_RETURN` `0x01`/`0x02` anchor committing `content_addr(assent/1)` at height `h_assent <= h_anchor` (`CONTRACT_ASSENT_INVALID` if neither is present) — preventing former guardians from backdating a forged ward assent to an old `0x07` contract after emancipation or resignation. Autonomous-only contracts MAY likewise use `assent_output` (`SIGHASH_ALL`) to prove mutual assent on chain to observers who never see contract plaintext.

### 2.3 The Contract Seal Decides Termination (`SL-CONT-005`, `decided`)

1. **Mandatory seal before signing:** Every framework contract and every contract under which any grant is issued MUST name a confirmed `seal` whose `script_policy` conforms to `ContractSealScriptPolicy` (`key`, `multisig`, `either_of_2`, `both_of_2`, or `arbiter_or_both`) created **before** parties sign `seallink-assent/1`. Signing services and verifiers MUST check confirmation and exact compiled `ScriptPolicy` match of both the bound contract's seal and any named framework contract's seal on their own node (`CONTRACT_SEAL_MISSING` on conclusive absence/mismatch; `SEAL_UNAVAILABLE` on inconclusive read).
2. **Script derives termination type — never prose:** Every reader, renderer, and UI MUST derive the contract's termination type strictly from `seal.script_policy` (`SL-LDGR-004`):
   - `{"kind":"key"}` or `{"kind":"multisig"}` -> **`issuer_only`**
   - `{"kind":"branch","template":"either_of_2"}` -> **`either_party`**
   - `{"kind":"branch","template":"both_of_2"}` -> **`both_together`**
   - `{"kind":"branch","template":"arbiter_or_both"}` -> **`arbiter`**
   A prose clause describing termination binds parties as text only and NEVER ends a contract or overrides the script-derived type.
3. **One spend ends all grants (`seallink-termination/1`):** Spending a contract or framework seal (`SEAL_SPENT`) durably terminates every grant bound to that contract (or framework) upon first observation and records a durable `contract` / `framework` closure. The spending transaction SHOULD carry `OP_RETURN` action `0x08` committing `typed_digest(seallink-termination/1)` (`SIGHASH_ALL`), where `seallink-termination/1` is signed EITHER: (a) under an autonomous party's `signing_policy`, (b) under the contract's designated arbiter key (the `arbiter` `BranchKeySpec` of `seal.script_policy` when `template == "arbiter_or_both"`, or `moderation.moderator_key` when `moderation` is present) when `termination_branch == "arbiter"`, OR (c) via `guardian_context` (`event_class = "contract_assent"`) + `guardian_approvals[]` (`SL-WIRE-013`, `SL-ORIG-006`) on behalf of a ward party (`termination_branch != "arbiter"`); however, the on-chain seal spend alone terminates the contract regardless of whether `0x08` is valid or present.

### 2.4 Agreed Configuration Hash (`SL-CONT-006`, `decided`)

Any contract profile under which a runtime launches a configured agent (`profiles/PROFILE-agent-v1.md`) MUST require `config_sha256`. A runtime launcher that cannot reproduce `config_sha256` from the exact profile, skills, and tools it is about to launch MUST refuse to start (`CONTRACT_CONFIG_MISMATCH`).

## 3. Violation Verdicts and Salted Request Attribution (`seallink-verdict/1`)

For moderated contracts (e.g., hosted services or agent sessions), `seallink-contract/1` MAY declare `moderation: {rules_sha256, moderator_key, moderator_kind}` (`SL-CONT-007`, `decided`):

1. Each recorded violation is a moderator-signed `seallink-verdict/1` chained via `ordinal` and `prev_verdict_id`, committing to `rules_sha256`, `violated_rule`, `request_salted_sha256`, and `response_salted_sha256`.
2. **Salted invocation attribution:** To allow dispute evidence without exposing plaintext on chain or enabling dictionary guesses against short messages, `seallink-invocation/1` MAY include a fresh 32-byte `content_salt` (distinct from `nonce`), with `salted_hash = SHA256(content_salt || plaintext_bytes)`.
3. A grant-seal revocation spend MAY include `OP_RETURN` action `0x03` committing `typed_digest(seallink-verdict/1)`. Closure still follows the seal spend alone; the verdict frame supplies auditable dispute evidence.

## 4. Verifiable Credentials (`seallink-credential/1`)

Third-party attestations about a subject — diplomas, licenses, notary credentials, human-verification claims (`subject-claim`), runtime isolation audits (`agent-audit`), and work-completion acts — share one object type: **`seallink-credential/1`** (`SL-CONT-008`, `decided`).

### 4.1 Lifecycles, Anchoring, and Compromise Cutoff `h_c`

1. **Signing role:** Signed ONLY by the issuer's `link.cred` role policy (`issuer.role_policy`, verified per `SL-IDEN-003` and `SL-WIRE-013`). A credential is NEVER a grant and confers zero execution authority by itself; verifier policies may only use credentials to narrow admission.
2. **Signed-form anchoring (`0x09` and `0x14`):** Every `eternal` or `revocable` credential MUST be anchored via a presenter-supplied confirmed `OP_RETURN` action `0x09` transaction (`anchor_txid`) on the active chain committing **`content_addr = SHA256(signed_bytes)`**, proving the signature existed at or before confirmed block `h_anchor` (`CRED_ANCHOR_MISSING`). Every `log_tracked` credential is batched inside a confirmed `OP_RETURN 0x14` transparency/denial log (`role = "cred"`, `§6`) committing `0x01 || content_addr(credential/1)` inside `batch_root`. Presenters SHOULD supply the earliest confirmed anchor transaction (because an earlier `h_anchor < h_c` strictly dominates a later anchor; Tier 1 Namecoin nodes do not maintain a global `OP_RETURN`-content index).
3. **Three lifecycles and subject binding modes (`key`, `name`, `origin`):**
   - **`eternal`:** MUST NOT carry `revocation_seal` or `log_id`; `not_after_height` / `not_after_unix` optional. Verified against the historical registration lineage matching `issuer.registration` at `h_anchor` (`link.cred` role policy active at `h_anchor`). Similarly, if `subject.mode = "name"`, `subject.name_pin` `(name, registration, owner_policy)` is verified at **`h_anchor`** (so an eternal diploma survives both issuer and subject name expiry/transfer). If `subject.mode = "origin"`, the presenter MUST supply a verified `origin_bundle` matching `subject.origin_id` (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` if omitted or invalid), and the verifier verifies **historical origin existence and trusted issuance** (`h_origin <= h_anchor`, confirmed `binding_seal` and `status_seal` UTXOs at `<= h_anchor` [failing closed `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` if missing/unconfirmed], valid `name_binding` if supplied, and `h_origin < h_c` under any origin-issuer `0x0b` break) — independent of whether an initial `0x0c` name binding had already confirmed at `h_anchor`, and independent of subsequent emancipation, status change, `seallink-end/1`, or name transfer.
   - **`revocable`:** MUST carry a confirmed `revocation_seal` (and MUST NOT carry `log_id`); `not_after_height` / `not_after_unix` optional. By default verifies the issuer's `link.cred` role policy, `subject.name_pin` (when `subject.mode = "name"`), and active `origin_bundle` status (when `subject.mode = "origin"`, `ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` if missing/invalid) current at tip (exceptions such as `seallink-guardian/1` verify issuer at `h_anchor`, `SL-ORIG-008`). Spending the seal closes the credential (`SEAL_SPENT`); spending with `OP_RETURN 0x0a` (`typed_digest(seallink-credential-withdrawal/1)`) records `CRED_REVOKED_FOR_CAUSE` (`seallink-credential-withdrawal/1` is an unsigned cause record authenticated by the on-chain spend of `revocation_seal`; when presented alongside `0x0a`, verifiers validate its closed schema and exact `credential_id` / `typed_digest` match against the `0x0a` frame; a `0x0a` spend without an off-chain document still revokes permanently and NEVER admits).
   - **`log_tracked`:** MUST carry `log_id` (and MUST NOT carry `revocation_seal`); `not_after_height` / `not_after_unix` optional. Batched inside an issuer's `role = "cred"` `0x14` transparency/denial log (`§6`, declared in `manifest.cred.log`), eliminating per-credential UTXO churn for high-volume issuers (universities, professional licensing boards, registries). Verified against `link.cred` and `subject` at the confirming checkpoint height `h_anchor` (bounded by `0x0b` `h_c` and `NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED`), checking the Fixed-Depth-256 SMT under denial key **`cred:<credential_id>|*`**: `"absent"` admits; `"included"` pointing to a valid `seallink-log-revocation/1` refuses **`CRED_REVOKED_FOR_CAUSE`**; `"included"` pointing to a valid `seallink-log-reinstatement/1` admits iff verifier policy sets `allow_reinstatement = true` (default `true`, else refuses `CRED_REVOKED_FOR_CAUSE`). Supports `O(1)` handover (`predecessor_chain`) and public `seallink-log-fraud/1` proofs identically to `notary` and `release` logs!
4. **Owner-authenticated compromise break `h_c` (`0x0b`, `SL-CONT-009`, `decided`):**
   - If an issuer's, notary's, release publisher's, or guardian's `cred`, `notary`, `grant`, or `release` role policy (current or retired) is compromised, the actor's cold `name_owner` `ScriptPolicy` broadcasts an `OP_NAME_UPDATE` (removing/replacing the role if current, signed `SIGHASH_ALL`) containing the 50-byte `0x0b` frame embedding `h_c_le64` (`<= inclusion_height`) and `typed_digest(seallink-credential-break/1)`.
   - Because verifiers reconstruct `seallink-credential-break/1` (`schemas/credential.schema.json#/$defs/CredentialBreak`, with `role ∈ {"cred", "notary", "grant", "release"}`) directly from the name history — using the confirmed spent previous name output `name_input` (whose trailing script MUST match the confirmed `owner_policy` active immediately prior to the `0x0b` update along the registration's continuous writer-verified history, `SL-LDGR-007`), `issuer: ActorRegistrationPin` (`{"name", "registration"}` strictly WITHOUT `owner_policy`, so `0x0b` survives planned cold `owner_policy` rotations `P1 -> P2` on the same registration! **Transfer honesty boundary (`D14-T01`):** `0x0b` is authenticated strictly by the registration's current on-chain owner, so SealLink actors MUST NEVER sell or transfer a name that issued historical `eternal` or `log_tracked` documents — doing so grants the buyer technical ability to emit a retroactive `0x0b` for prior keys on that registration; cold owner-key rotation by the same controller is safe and preserves `0x0b` reachability), `role`, candidate historical canonical `compromised_policy` (`ThresholdPolicy` without `"alg"`), and on-chain `h_c` — withholding off-chain break documents cannot hide a compromise.
   - For the exact affected `(issuer.name, issuer.registration, role, compromised_policy)` whose reconstructed `typed_digest(seallink-credential-break/1)` matches the `0x0b` frame commitment: anchors at `h_anchor < h_c` (e.g., `h_anchor = h_c - 1`) **survive**, while anchors at `h_anchor >= h_c` (`h_anchor = h_c` or `h_anchor = h_c + 1`) durably refuse **`CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`** (for eternal and revocable credentials, notary acts, release records, AND both historical and live-tip ward `grant` approvals). Multiple breaks matching the same policy narrow by `min(h_c)`. Durable `role` closures in `FencedJournal` MUST be keyed by **`(cause, name, registration, role, policy_sha256)`** (`schemas/invocation.schema.json#/$defs/ClosureSubject`) — separating `CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED` (`cutoff_height = h_c`) from routine `NAME_ROLE_CHANGED` (`retired_before_height`) so a later `0x0b` NEVER overwrites or weakens an earlier `NAME_ROLE_CHANGED` closure, scoping the closure to that exact `(name, registration, role, policy_sha256)` even across subsequent `owner_policy` changes on the same registration, and writing idempotently (zero duplicate writes on repeated observation). Observing a late anchor `h_late >= h_c` NEVER blocks an earlier anchor `h_early < h_c` of the same document or a distinct successor policy `B` installed via valid `OP_NAME_UPDATE` in the same journal. *(Because `0x0b` is an on-chain owner-authenticated ledger fact reconstructed directly from confirmed name history rather than caller-asserted metadata, recording a verified `CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED` cutoff upon witnessing a confirmed `0x0b` frame does not depend on validating an unauthenticated caller's signature.)*
5. **Privacy:** Raw PII MUST NOT appear on chain (`loc_type = 0x00` default). Issuers deduplicating humans MUST NOT hash raw biometrics directly. Per-claim selective disclosure uses `claim_commitments` with an independent 32-byte CSPRNG salt per entry (`SL-WIRE-014`).

### 4.2 Deterministic Credential Verification Order (`SL-CONT-010`, `decided`)

1. Wire pipeline (`SPEC-01 §7`) -> `WIRE_*`
2. Supported `credential_kind` -> `WIRE_TYPE`; claim schema compliance -> `WIRE_MEMBER`
3. Chain/genesis match -> `CHAIN_MISMATCH`
4. For `eternal`/`revocable`: presenter-supplied confirmed signed-form `0x09` anchor present on active chain -> `CRED_ANCHOR_MISSING`; confirmation depth `>= required_depth` -> `CHAIN_DEPTH_INSUFFICIENT`; for `log_tracked`: verified `0x14` `role = "cred"` checkpoint inclusion, ancestor chain, durable log closure (`NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED`), and canonical SMT denial proof (`cred:<credential_id>|*`, `§6`, refusing `CRED_REVOKED_FOR_CAUSE` on active revocation or disallowed reinstatement)
5. Subject verification: if `subject.mode = "name"`, verify `subject.name_pin` at `h_anchor` (`eternal`/`log_tracked`) or tip (`revocable`) -> `NAME_OWNER_MISMATCH` / `NAME_NOT_FOUND`; if `subject.mode = "origin"`, require a verified `origin_bundle` matching `subject.origin_id` (`historical_existence_only` with `h_origin <= h_anchor` for `eternal`/`log_tracked`, without requiring initial `0x0c` binding to exist at `h_anchor`; live origin status for `revocable`) -> `ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED`
6. Issuer `link.cred` history walk available -> `NAME_HISTORY_UNAVAILABLE`; `owner_policy` & `role_policy` match (at `h_anchor` for `eternal`/`log_tracked` or tip for `revocable`) -> `NAME_OWNER_MISMATCH` / `WIRE_SIGNATURE`; `0x0b` compromise check (`h_anchor >= h_c` refuses; `h_anchor < h_c` survives) -> `CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`
7. Height window (`not_before_height <= H < not_after_height`) AND calendar window (`now < not_after_unix` when present) -> `CRED_WINDOW_CLOSED`
8. Persisted journal `credential` closure check AND (for `revocable`) revocable seal state (`0x0a` cause -> `CRED_REVOKED_FOR_CAUSE`; plain spend -> `SEAL_SPENT`; unavailable -> `SEAL_UNAVAILABLE`)

## 5. General Notary Acts (`seallink-notary/1`)

A notary is an independent off-authority-path actor (human, organization, or agent; refusing by actor class is invalid unless required by explicit policy) acting under its `link.notary` role policy (`SL-CONT-011`, `decided`):

1. **Six act kinds (each with a closed `details` schema bound strictly to `act_kind == details.kind` in `schemas/credential.schema.json`):**
   - `contract-notarization`: certifies that named parties executed `contract_id` (plus optional `notary_clause_sha256`).
   - `certified-copy`: certifies correspondence of `original_sha256` and `media_type` to an inspected original.
   - `fact-attestation`: certifies a witnessed `fact_sha256` under `fact_schema` at `(tip_height, tip_hash)`.
   - `child-within-parent`: optional re-delegation attestation naming `(parent_grant_id, child_grant_id, ordinal)` when parent policy demands prose/policy review alongside mechanical narrowing.
   - `artifact-notarization`: certifies claims (`claims[]`, each with `claim_id` and `verdict ∈ {"pass", "fail", "inconclusive"}`) over a canonical `artifact_identity` (`schemas/log.schema.json#/$defs/ArtifactIdentity`), requiring `target_sha256 == H_tag("seallink-artifact-id/1", JCS({"digest", "scheme"}))` strictly excluding `params` (`SL-CONT-013`).
   - `contract-ruling`: certifies an independent arbitration, dispute-panel, or judicial ruling over a `seallink-contract/1` (`contract_id`, optional strictly ascending unique `disclosed_clause_indices[]`, `ruling_sha256`, and `outcome ∈ {"upheld", "breach", "terminated", "dismissed", "modified"}`), requiring `target_sha256 == details.contract_id` (`WIRE_MEMBER`) and strictly ascending `disclosed_clause_indices` (`WIRE_INTEGER`) — enabling verifiable public or private dispute outcomes without forcing disclosure of unrelated confidential contract clauses.
2. **Lifecycles & Anchoring (`0x04` and `0x14`):** Supports THREE lifecycles: **`eternal`** (`0x04`, no `notary_seal`, verified against `link.notary` at `h_anchor`, bounded by `0x0b` `h_c`), **`revocable`** (`0x04` + `notary_seal`; plain spend withdraws allowing recertification; spend with `0x05` committing `typed_digest(seallink-credential-withdrawal/1)` records `CRED_REVOKED_FOR_CAUSE`), and **`log_tracked`** (`log_id` required, `notary_seal` forbidden; batched inside a `0x14` transparency/denial log checkpoint per `§6` with SMT denial key `act:<act_id>|*` and optional `target:<target_id>|*` veto).
3. **Notary identity pin & deterministic verification order:** Every reference to a notary pins `(name, registration, owner_policy)` with mandatory writer verification (`SL-LDGR-007`) so transferring a notary's name never transfers notarizing authority. Verifying any `seallink-notary/1` MUST execute in order:
   1. Wire pipeline (`SPEC-01 §7`) & `network`/`genesis` match (`CHAIN_MISMATCH`) & canonical `target_sha256` check for `artifact-notarization` (`WIRE_MEMBER`) and `contract-ruling` (`target_sha256 == details.contract_id` -> `WIRE_MEMBER`; strictly ascending `disclosed_clause_indices` -> `WIRE_INTEGER`);
   2. For `eternal`/`revocable`: presenter-supplied confirmed `0x04` anchor matching `content_addr(seallink-notary/1)` (`CRED_ANCHOR_MISSING`) at depth `>= required_depth` (`CHAIN_DEPTH_INSUFFICIENT`); for `log_tracked`: verify `0x14` checkpoint inclusion, ancestor chain, durable log closure (`NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED`), and canonical SMT denial proof (`§6`);
   3. Notary `link.notary` history walk & writer-verified `owner_policy` match at `h_anchor` (`eternal`/`log_tracked`) or tip (`revocable`) (`NAME_HISTORY_UNAVAILABLE` / `NAME_OWNER_MISMATCH` / `WIRE_SIGNATURE`) + `0x0b` compromise check (`h_anchor >= h_c` refuses `CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`; `h_anchor < h_c` survives);
   4. Persisted journal `credential` closure check AND (for `revocable`) `notary_seal` state (`0x05` -> `CRED_REVOKED_FOR_CAUSE`; plain spend -> `SEAL_SPENT`; unavailable -> `SEAL_UNAVAILABLE`);
   5. When required by a parent grant (`notary_mode != "none"`): `act_kind == "child-within-parent"`, exact `(parent_grant_id, child_grant_id, ordinal)` equality, and exact notary `NamePin` membership in `parent.delegate.notaries[]` (plus `notary.name == parent.delegate.notary_name` when configured; `NOTARY_REQUIRED` otherwise).

## 6. Transparency & Denial Logs (`0x14 LOG_CHECKPOINT`), Canonical SMT & Fraud Proofs (`SL-CONT-012`, `decided`)

To support high-volume notarization, content/software release publishing, and verifiable credential issuance without per-item UTXO churn, SealLink Core defines ONE single-use-seal transparency and denial log (`schemas/log.schema.json`) shared uniformly across **`role ∈ {"notary", "release", "cred"}`**:

1. **Immutable `log_id` (folding `predecessor_log_id` AND `predecessor_terminal_denials_root`) & unconditional issuer binding (`D14-B07`):**
   - `log_id = H_tag("seallink-log-id/1", JCS({"genesis", "genesis_seal_outpoint": {"txid", "vout"}, "issuer_name", "issuer_registration", "network", "role", ["predecessor_log_id", "predecessor_terminal_denials_root"]}))` (where `"predecessor_log_id"` and `"predecessor_terminal_denials_root"` are included together in the JCS object strictly iff the log hands over from a predecessor log, so BOTH the predecessor identity AND its exact terminal `denials_root` are cryptographically immutable inside `log_id` across ALL `seq >= 1`, making revocation-wipe at `seq >= 2` mathematically impossible!).
   - Every `seallink-log-checkpoint/1` (for ALL `seq >= 1`) and every `seallink-log-fraud/1` carries a REQUIRED **`genesis_seal_outpoint`** (`Outpoint`) plus optional `predecessor_log_id` (present iff the log is a handover successor; `predecessor_terminal_denials_root` is required in the payload at `seq == 1` iff `predecessor_log_id` is present, and forbidden at `seq >= 2` because it is already authenticated via `predecessor_chain` / `journal.log_heads` and bound inside `log_id`). Verifiers MUST authenticate the predecessor chain iteratively in $O(n)$ (`<= 256` hops, zero recursion) to obtain `pred_term_dr`, recompute `expected_log_id = compute_log_id(network, genesis, role, issuer.name, issuer.registration, genesis_seal_outpoint, predecessor_log_id, predecessor_terminal_denials_root=pred_term_dr)`, and verify `expected_log_id == log_id` on EVERY checkpoint and fraud proof (`CHAIN_FRAME_INVALID` on mismatch, checked BEFORE any journal write).
   - Cross-height log consistency (`checkpoint` vs `head_checkpoint` vs `item` vs `state_record` vs `predecessor`) matches issuer identity strictly on **`(issuer.name, issuer.registration)`** (NOT exact `owner_policy`) so routine cold `owner_policy` rotation on a notary/release name never breaks historical log entries or checkpoints (`issuer.owner_policy` is still verified against confirmed name history at each object's own confirmation height `h`).
2. **Canonical `0x14` Output Layout (Continuation, On-Chain Handover, & EOL `P2A` Sentinel), Single-Use-Seal Chain Induction, Head Freshness & Maturing-Successor Grace (`D14-B07`, `D14-B12`):**
   - **Canonical `0x14` transaction layout (eliminating fee-change / shadow-branch bypasses while preserving CPFP on permanent EOL):**
     - `vout 0`: ALWAYS zero-value `SLNK 0x14 LOG_CHECKPOINT` `OP_RETURN` committing `content_addr(seallink-log-checkpoint/1)` signed by `link.<role>`.
     - When `terminal == false`: **`vout 1` is ALWAYS `next_log_outpoint`** (`next_log_vout: const 1`, locked strictly to `compile(next_log_policy)`, NEVER `P2A`); fee change and optional `P2A` anchors MUST be placed at `vout >= 2`.
     - When `terminal == true` **with `O(1)` handover (`handover_next_vout: const 1` + `handover_next_policy` present):** `vout 1` is the **on-chain handover seal** (`compile(handover_next_policy)`, NEVER `P2A`). Successor log `L2` MUST set `L2.genesis_seal_outpoint == (T_L1_handover, 1)`. Once `L2`'s first `0x14` checkpoint matures, `(T_L1_handover, 1)` is spent — killing both frozen-terminal `L1` replay and parallel successor forks (`L2a`/`L2b`) by Bitcoin/Namecoin UTXO consensus!
     - When `terminal == true` **permanent end-of-life (EOL, no `handover_next_policy`):** EITHER **`len(vout) == 1`** (`vout 0` `OP_RETURN` only) OR **`vout 0` `OP_RETURN` + `vout 1 = P2A (51024e73)` + optional `vout 2` change** (`P2A` is unconditionally rejected as a `0x14` continuation in `_verify_0x14_utxo_chain`, making EOL 100% distinguishable on chain while preserving CPFP fee-bumping and change!).
   - **Head freshness (`is_head = true`) & maturing-successor grace:** Whenever a non-terminal (`terminal == false`) OR handover-terminal (`handover_next_policy` present) checkpoint is verified as the current log head (`is_head = true`), the verifier MUST confirm that `(cp_txid, 1)` is NOT spent by a matured (`depth >= required_depth`) or non-`0x14` transaction (and NOT `absent_from_utxo` without a known spending tx). If `(cp_txid, 1)` is spent strictly by a live mempool (`in_mempool == true`) or maturing (`depth < required_depth`) `0x14` successor transaction, the previous confirmed head MAY continue to be accepted during the maturation window; once a `0x14` successor reaches `required_depth` (or any non-`0x14` spend occurs), the superseded head MUST refuse **`CHAIN_FRAME_INVALID`** (`TICKET_PROOF_INVALID` in Gatekeeper tickets — identically on fresh and stateful verifiers!).
   - **`log_seal` single-use-seal chain induction (`seq >= 2`) & handover cadence (`<= 4096`):** Every `seq >= 2` checkpoint MUST authenticate its `prev_log_outpoint` backwards via memoized DFS across inputs spending `prev_vout == 1` (immune to dust-input poisoning on extra fee inputs!) through confirmed active-chain `0x14` transactions (rejecting `P2A 51024e73` as a continuation) until reaching `ex_head` [checking `!ex_head.terminal`, exact `ex_head.next_log_outpoint`, `ex_head.checkpoint_id`, and `compile(ex_head.next_log_policy)`] or `genesis_seal_outpoint` (`CHAIN_FRAME_INVALID`). Operators SHOULD execute an `O(1)` handover at least every **`<= 4096` checkpoints** (matching `ColdNamecoinObserver.max_log_chain_hops = 4096`) so fresh verifiers bound backward walk depth cleanly. Whenever `prev_cp_env` (`prev_cp_txid`) is supplied, verifiers additionally enforce `!prev.terminal`, `prev_log_outpoint == (prev_txid, 1)`, `compile(prev.next_log_policy)`, `cumulative_entries_count`, and `checkpoints_root`. **History-rewrite defense (`D14-M06`):** stateful verifiers observing a jump from `ex_head.max_seq` to a higher `seq` SHOULD validate the intervening `prev_cp_env` or RFC 9162 consistency proof (`peaks[]`) so a withheld intermediate checkpoint cannot rewrite earlier `checkpoints_root` history without triggering Mode 4 `invalid_checkpoint_chain` fraud.
   - **`batch_root` & `denials_delta_root`:** Constructed via `SPEC-02 §5.3` over tagged leaves `H_tag("seallink-log-entry/1", kind_byte || content_addr_32B)` (`0x01` issue, `0x02` revocation, `0x03` reinstatement), or `H_tag("seallink-batch-empty/1", b"")` when empty (`batch_count == 0` requires both roots empty).
   - **Acyclic RFC 9162 `checkpoints_root` (strictly over predecessors `1..(seq - 1)`):** At `seq == 1`, `checkpoints_root == H_tag("seallink-log-ckpt-empty/1", b"")`. At `seq = k >= 2`, `checkpoints_root == MTH([C_0, ..., C_{k-2}])` per RFC 9162 §2.1.1 where `C_i = H_tag("seallink-log-ckpt-leaf/1", uint64_be(i + 1) || content_addr_32B(cp_{i+1}))` and `M(L, R) = H_tag("seallink-log-ckpt-node/1", L || R)`.
   - **Authenticated `O(1)` log handover & portable `predecessor_chain` (`<= 256` hops, iterative $O(n)$):** When `predecessor_log_id` is present, `predecessor_terminal_denials_root` is REQUIRED at `seq == 1` (forbidden at `seq >= 2`), `cp_1` MUST satisfy `denials_root == predecessor_terminal_denials_root` and `denials_delta_root == H_tag("seallink-batch-empty/1", b"")`, AND the verifier MUST iteratively authenticate every predecessor log in the handover chain bottom-up (via portable `predecessor_chain` [`PredecessorTerminalCheckpoint[]`, `maxItems: 256`, each confirmed `terminal == true` with `handover_next_policy` and `child.genesis_seal_outpoint == (T_pred, 1)`] or persisted `terminal == true` `journal.log_heads[predecessor_log_id]` matching `predecessor_log_id`, `role`, `handover_next_outpoint`, `(issuer_name, issuer_registration)`, and `denials_root`, while refusing `NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED` if any predecessor `log_id` is closed). Inherited SMT state records and historical `log_tracked` items signed under any authenticated predecessor `log_id` of the same `(issuer.name, issuer.registration)` remain verifiable against the successor log's `denials_root`.
   - **Reorg-safe journal persistence (`log-head`) & durable equivocation closure:** Verifiers persist confirmed heads in `FencedJournal` as `JournalEntry(kind = "log-head")` (`SPEC-06 §6`, recording mandatory `issuer_name`, `issuer_registration`, `checkpoint_id = content_addr(cp)`, `checkpoint_object_id = object_id(cp.payload)`, `block_hash`, `confirmed_height`, `terminal`, `next_log_outpoint`/`next_log_policy` when `terminal == false`, and `handover_next_outpoint`/`handover_next_policy` when `terminal == true` with handover) strictly after reaching `required_depth` and confirming `cp_tx.block_hash` (missing `block_hash` refuses `CHAIN_UNHEALTHY`). Same `seq == max_seq` with `object_id(cp.payload) != ex_head.checkpoint_object_id` (excluding identical log state differing solely in RBF `prev_log_outpoint`) MUST **immediately persist durable `NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED` closure** to `FencedJournal` (and SHOULD archive the conflicting checkpoint envelope for exportable Mode 1 `equivocation` fraud evidence, `D14-M06`) before refusing **`NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED`**; conversely, if `ex_head.block_hash` was genuinely orphaned in a reorg and the **exact same `checkpoint_object_id`** re-confirms in a new `txid`/`block_hash`, the verifier atomically updates `log-head` to the new `(txid, block_hash, next_log_outpoint)` so valid successors continue cleanly! Stale `seq < max_seq` refuses non-closing **`CHAIN_FRAME_INVALID`** (`TICKET_PROOF_INVALID` in Gatekeeper tickets). When `terminal == true` without handover (permanent EOL), heartbeat freshness stops while the final `denials_root` remains valid permanently.
3. **Canonical Fixed-Depth-256 Logical Binary SMT (`denials_root`), Role-Scoped `DenialKeyStr`, & Historical `StateRecordInclusion`:**
   - Key hash `k = SHA256(UTF8(denial_key))` over the closed v1.0 4-key universe **`act:<object_id(notary_act.payload)>|*`**, **`rel:<object_id(release.payload)>|*`**, **`cred:<object_id(credential.payload)>|*`**, and **`target:<target_sha256>|*`** (`schemas/log.schema.json#/$defs/DenialKeyStr`, `maxLength: 80`), **strictly role-scoped** in `LogRevocation` and `LogReinstatement`: `role == "release"` permits ONLY `rel:<id>|*`, `role == "cred"` permits ONLY `cred:<id>|*`, while `role == "notary"` permits ONLY `act:<id>|*` or `target:<id>|*` (`WIRE_MEMBER` at Step 8 on prefix/role mismatch). Uses MSB-first bit order (`d = 0` at root, `d = 255` at leaf), empty defaults `D[0] = H_tag("seallink-smt-empty/1", b"")`, `D[h] = H_tag("seallink-smt-node/1", D[h-1] || D[h-1])`, leaf `L(k, v) = H_tag("seallink-smt-leaf/1", k || v_32B)`.
   - Every `SmtProof` carries MSB-first `bitmap_hex` (32 B) and bottom-up `siblings[]` (`h = 1..256`, `len == popcount(bitmap_hex)`), enforcing canonical **`sib != D[h - 1]`** for every `1`-bit.
   - `seallink-log-revocation/1` (`state_seq >= 1`, `0x02`) and `seallink-log-reinstatement/1` (`state_seq >= 2`, `0x03`) both require `prev_record_id` iff `state_seq >= 2` (forbidding it at `state_seq == 1`), supporting audited `revoke -> reinstate -> revoke` transitions while forbidding deletion back to `D[0]`.
   - **Historical key-rotation & handover survival (`StateRecordInclusion`):** Whenever an SMT leaf `state_record` (revocation or reinstatement) was signed under a previously active `link.<role>` key rotated *before* `head_checkpoint`'s height `h_head` (or introduced in an authenticated predecessor log), the presenter MAY supply `state_record_inclusion: StateRecordInclusion` (`{checkpoint, checkpoint_txid, batch_proof, checkpoints_proof?}`) alongside `predecessor_chain` proving `state_record` entered `batch_root` (`0x02` for revocation, `0x03` for reinstatement) in an ancestor checkpoint `cp_intro` confirmed at `h_intro <= h_head` (included in `head_checkpoint.checkpoints_root` when in the same log, or matching an authenticated predecessor log checkpoint). Verifiers then verify `state_record`'s `link.<role>` signature and `0x0b` cutoff strictly at **`h_intro`** (falling back to `h_head` when `state_record_inclusion` is omitted and the key was not rotated), propagating `CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`, `NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED`, `JOURNAL_LOST`, and `JOURNAL_FENCED` unconditionally.
4. **4-Mode `seallink-log-fraud/1` & Unified 100% `CLOSE` (`D14-B07`, `D14-B12`, `D14-B13`):**
   - Unsigned publicly verifiable fraud proof (requiring `genesis_seal_outpoint` and supporting optional `predecessor_log_id` + `predecessor_chain` across **ALL 4 modes**, performing ZERO journal mutations until fraud is completely proven and failing closed `CRED_ANCHOR_MISSING` whenever a supplied `cp_*_txid` fails `0x14` anchor verification without falling back to tip) supporting 4 discriminated modes (`fraud_mode`):
     1. `"equivocation"`: two valid checkpoints for identical `(log_id, seq)` whose log-state payloads (stripping `prev_log_outpoint` so legitimate RBF fee-bumping of `T_{seq-1}` with identical log state NEVER triggers false equivocation!) differ (confirmed `cp_*_txid` verified against `link.<role>` at `h_conf`; unconfirmed/offline checkpoint verified against live `link.<role>` at tip with `0x0b` check).
     2. `"revocation_omission"`: confirmed `cp_a` (in `log_id` OR an authenticated predecessor log via `predecessor_chain`) includes `0x02 || content_addr(revocation)` in `batch_root`, `cp_b` (in `log_id`) is `cp_a` or a descendant (proven via `checkpoints_proof` or handover `predecessor_chain`), yet `cp_b.denials_root` EITHER proves `revocation.denial_key` `"absent"` OR proves `"included"` at a stale record `rec_b` (`rec_b.log_id` in authenticated log lineage) with `rec_b.state_seq < revocation.state_seq`.
     3. `"illegal_reversion"`: confirmed `cp_a` (in `log_id` with `seq_a < seq_b` proven in `cp_b.checkpoints_root`, OR in an authenticated predecessor log via `predecessor_chain` with `checkpoints_proof` omitted iff `cp_a` is the predecessor's terminal checkpoint) commits `rec_a` in `cp_a.denials_root` (authenticated strictly by `smt_proof_a` and structural field match), while `cp_b` either **(3a)** deletes the key to `"absent"`, **(3b)** rolls back/substitutes `rec_b.state_seq <= rec_a.state_seq` (`content_addr(rec_b) != content_addr(rec_a)`), **(3c)** breaks adjacency (`rec_b.state_seq == rec_a.state_seq + 1` with `rec_b.prev_record_id != content_addr(rec_a)`), or **(3d)** commits a **different** (`content_addr(rec_b) != content_addr(rec_a)`) invalid successor `rec_b` whose signature, role policy, or `0x0b` compromise fails **conclusively** at its exact introduction height `h_intro`. **Strict anti-kill-switch rule for `(3d)` (`D14-B13`):** (i) `rec_b_inclusion.checkpoint.payload.log_id` MUST equal `log_id`, and any invalid or malformed `rec_b_inclusion` MUST immediately refuse the fraud proof with `CHAIN_FRAME_INVALID` and zero journal writes; (ii) evaluating `(3d)` requires EITHER same-log adjacent checkpoints (`cp_a.log_id == log_id` and `seq_b == seq_a + 1`) OR a validly verified `rec_b_inclusion` (`seq_a < seq_intro <= seq_b` within `log_id`, or `1 <= seq_intro <= seq_b` for cross-handover `cp_a`); (iii) ONLY conclusive cryptographic/role refusals (`WIRE_SIGNATURE`, `NAME_OWNER_MISMATCH`, `NAME_ROLE_CHANGED`, `CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`, `NAME_NOT_FOUND`, `NAME_VALUE_INVALID`) prove `(3d)` fraud — any transient or incomplete error (e.g., `NAME_HISTORY_UNAVAILABLE`, `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE`, `CHAIN_UNHEALTHY`) MUST refuse the fraud proof with that error and ZERO log closure!
     4. `"invalid_checkpoint_chain"`: confirmed `cp_next` violates `seq == 1` empty `checkpoints_root` / `cumulative_entries_count == batch_count` / `O(1)` handover invariants (including `predecessor_terminal_denials_root != authenticated pred_term_dr`), OR at `seq = k >= 2` (given confirmed `cp_prev` and `peaks[]` of length `popcount(k - 2)` matching `cp_prev.checkpoints_root`) violates `append_rfc9162_leaf(peaks, k - 2, C_{k-2}) == cp_next.checkpoints_root` or `cumulative_entries_count == prev + batch_count`.
   - Any verified `seallink-log-fraud/1` records durable `ClosureSubject(subject_kind = "log", log_id)` with cause **`NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED`**, unconditionally blocking **100%** of acts, releases, and credentials of `log_id` across all verifier modes.

## 7. Artifact Identity and Content / Software Release Records (`seallink-release/1`, `SL-CONT-013`, `decided`)

1. **Canonical `target_id` (`registry/artifact-schemes.json`):** For every `ArtifactIdentity`, `target_id = target_sha256 = H_tag("seallink-artifact-id/1", JCS({"digest", "scheme"}))` strictly excluding `params` (preventing scheme-parameter split-identity attacks). Standard schemes include `sha256`, `fsverity-sha256` (Linux kernel `FS_IOC_MEASURE_VERITY`: `SHA256(256-byte little-endian fsverity_descriptor)` with `version = 1` [1B], `hash_algorithm = 1` [1B], `log_blocksize = 12` [1B, `4096`B], `salt_size = 0` [1B], `__reserved_0x04 = 0` [4B LE], `data_size` [8B LE], 32-byte Merkle root over 4096-byte zero-padded blocks [or `00*32` for empty file], and 208 trailing zero bytes; empty-file digest `3d248ca542a24fc62d1c43b916eae5016878e2533c88238480b26128a1f1af95`, and JSON `params = {"version": 1, "hash_alg": "sha256", "block_size": 4096, "salt_hex": ""}`), `oci-digest-sha256`, `git-tree-sha256`, `apple-cdhash-sha256`, and namespaced reverse-DNS schemes (e.g., `works.relux.curator/content-v2`).
2. **`seallink-release/1` (`schemas/log.schema.json`):** Actor-agnostic and content-agnostic versioned publication record signed strictly under `link.release` (`issuer.role_policy`) — equally applicable to software binaries, firmware, containers, books, scientific datasets, media editions, legal publications, and AI model weights — binding `(product_id, version_str, release_seq, artifact_identity, target_sha256, lifecycle ∈ {"eternal", "log_tracked"}, released_at)` (plus `log_id` iff `log_tracked`). `eternal` releases anchor via `0x09`; `log_tracked` releases anchor via `0x14` (`role = "release"`, denial key `rel:<release_id>|*`). Subject to `0x0b` `CredentialBreak(role = "release")` and `NOTARY_LOG_COMPROMISED`.

## 8. Security Considerations

- **Choosing `h_c`:** Setting `h_c` too late preserves earlier attacker forgeries; setting `h_c` too early invalidates legitimate historical documents.
- **Selective clause context:** Disclosing a single contract clause without qualifying clauses can mislead; `machine_clause_indices` ensures executors always see every machine-enforced clause, while arbiters MAY demand additional prose clauses before ruling.
- **Notary truth limit:** A notary act cryptographically binds the notary's accountability to a claim; it never proves physical truth mathematically.
- **Log fee-bumping:** Log operators MUST use CPFP (or RBF committing the identical `seallink-log-checkpoint/1` payload) so fee-bumping never creates an `equivocation` fraud proof.
