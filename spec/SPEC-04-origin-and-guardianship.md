## 1. The Three-Record Separation Model and Authenticated Status Spines

A single "who owns whom" record fails when a parent loses parental rights or an agent changes guardians, because a verifier must independently establish: (1) the origin certificate remains permanently authentic; (2) the ledger name still belongs to the subject; (3) the subject has neither emancipated nor ended; and (4) the presenting guardian(s) currently hold live slots meeting the event quorum.

SealLink therefore separates origin, status, binding, roster, and per-slot guardianship into cleanly linked UTXO-backed lines (`SL-ORIG-001`, `decided`):

| Record | Object Type | Anchor / UTXO Spine | Question Answered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Origin Certificate** | `seallink-origin/1` | Eternal anchor `0x09` + declares `binding_seal`, `status_seal`, and optional `roster_seal` (`disposer_pk`, `arbiter_pk`, `challenger_pk`) | Who certified immutable `origin_class` (`person \| agent \| organization`), salted initial guardians, quorum policy, and `name_recovery`? |
| **2. Status Spine** | `seallink-capacity/1`, `seallink-end/1` | Spends `status_seal` with `0x09` (`capacity/1` continues spine via `next_status_vout`; `end/1` is terminal) | While `status_seal` is unspent, proves **neither emancipation nor subject end has occurred** without global chain scanning. |
| **3. Name Binding** | `seallink-name-binding/1` | Spends `binding_seal` (or `recovery_seal`, continuing via `next_recovery_vout`) with `0x0c` | Which ledger registration `(name, registration_outpoint)` and `owner_policy` belong to `origin_id`? |
| **4. Roster Revision** | `seallink-guardian-roster/1` | Spends `roster_seal` (`both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)`) with `0x0f` | What is the current approved `revision`, `revision_size`, role keys (`disposer_pk`, `arbiter_pk`, `challenger_pk`), and valid slot set (including recovery of burned slots)? |
| **5. Slot Guardianship** | `seallink-guardian/1` | Per-slot UTXO chain (`0x12` direct initial activation OR `0x0d` Stage 1 pending -> `0x0e` Stage 2 active) | Who occupies slot `i`, under what term, and is that slot active? |

**Identity permanence:** Identity rests on the eternal `origin_id` and its verifiable UTXO spines, not on the mutable ledger name string.

## 2. Origin Certificates, Privacy Commitments, and Status Spine (`seallink-origin/1`)

### 2.1 Origin-Certified Class and Private Commitments (`SL-ORIG-002`, `decided`)

1. Subject class (`person`, `agent`, `organization`) is established **solely** by a verified `seallink-origin/1` signed by an issuer `cred` policy whose complete **`NamePin`** (`name`, `registration`, `owner_policy`) is pinned in local verifier configuration (`trusted_origin_issuer_pins: list[NamePin]`, never bare name strings alone). **Untrusted-certifier constraints-only mode:** when a verifier evaluates an origin-bound name whose `seallink-origin/1` issuer is NOT in `trusted_origin_issuer_pins`, it MUST NOT treat the subject as certified (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` when certified class is required), and MUST still fail closed rather than letting an unverified ward bypass guardianship via a missing or untrusted `origin_bundle`. **Presentation containers (`origin_bundle` and `registered_bindings`):** an `origin_bundle` is a presentation-layer bundle carrying `{origin, origin_txid, name_binding, recovery_bindings?, voluntary_rebinds?, guardian_records?, roster_records?, capacity_record?, end_record?, card?, opened_majority_unix?, majority_salt_hex?}` verified entirely against confirmed on-chain seals and `OP_RETURN` anchors (never trusted on its own); `registered_bindings` is a local verifier index mapping `"<txid>:<vout>"` registration outpoints to confirmed `0x0c` `origin_id`s observed on chain so cold verifiers detect ward/origin status even if a later update strips `link.origin`.
2. Self-declared `seallink-card/1` fields prove nothing; if a card is present for a verified origin, `card.kind` MUST match `origin_class` AND `card.name` MUST match the verified bound name (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` on mismatch).
3. Two conflicting trusted origin certificates for one registration MUST refuse (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED`). Absence of an origin certificate yields class `unverified`. An `organization` origin MUST NOT declare `guardian_policy` (`WIRE_MEMBER`). Whenever `guardian_policy` is present, `name_recovery.mode` MUST be `"dual_party_delayed"` (`WIRE_MEMBER`), ensuring guardian key loss or refusal at majority can never permanently lock a ward's identity.
4. **Pre-registration issuance:** `seallink-origin/1` is signed and anchored *before* name registration (`h_origin < h_firstupdate`), identifying the subject by `subject_initial_key` and committing `claims.intended_name` and `claims.intended_owner_policy`. All seals declared in `origin/1` (`binding_seal`, `status_seal`, optional `roster_seal`, optional `recovery_seal`, and every `guardian_policy.slots[i].vacant_utxo`) MUST have pairwise-distinct outpoints, MUST be confirmed on the active chain, and MUST match their compiled `ScriptPolicy` (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` / `CHAIN_SCRIPT_UNSUPPORTED` / `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` otherwise).
5. **Privacy of birth and initial guardians:** `seallink-origin/1` MUST NOT store plaintext `majority_unix` or plaintext initial guardian names:
   - `claims.majority_commitment` is **REQUIRED** for `origin_class = "person"` with `guardian_policy`, and **FORBIDDEN** for `origin_class = "agent"` or `"organization"` (`WIRE_MEMBER`). It commits to `{"majority_unix": T}` using salted selective disclosure with an independent 32-byte CSPRNG salt (`SL-WIRE-014`). Whenever `claims.majority_commitment` is present in `origin/1`, verifiers MUST require off-chain disclosure `(salt, {"majority_unix": T})` before admitting any guardian action: withheld disclosure refuses **`ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE`**; a presented opening whose computed hash mismatches `claims.majority_commitment` refuses **`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED`**.
   - Each `guardian_policy.slots[i].initial_guardian_commitment` commits to the initial guardian's complete `NamePin` (`{name, registration, owner_policy}`) using salted selective disclosure with its own independent 32-byte CSPRNG salt (`SL-WIRE-014`). Initial activation (`0x12`) opens `(salt_i, initial_guardian_NamePin)` and verifies exact commitment equality (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` on mismatch).
   - **Optional `claims.identity_commitments` (max `64` entries):** Any origin class (`person`, `agent`, `organization`) MAY carry a map of named salted commitments `key -> H_tag("seallink-clause/1", salt_32B || canonical_JCS(claim_obj))` (`SL-WIRE-014`) binding private external identifiers (such as Proof-of-Personhood attestations, civil-registry / D-U-N-S / LEI numbers, or corporate charter digests) without exposing raw PII on the ledger, enabling selective disclosure and catastrophic-loss identity continuity.
6. **Authenticated status discovery, exact authority pins, and continuation (`status_seal`):** `seallink-origin/1` MUST pin a confirmed `status_seal` whose controlling `ScriptPolicy` keys belong exclusively to the designated `capacity_authority` / `end_authority` (defaulting to `origin.issuer` when omitted). Every status-spine and guardianship record MUST match its authorized `NamePin` (`name`, `registration`, `owner_policy`) **exactly**:
   - `seallink-capacity/1` `issuer` MUST equal `origin.capacity_authority` (or `origin.issuer` if `capacity_authority` is omitted), and its `status_prev_outpoint` MUST equal the exact current `status_seal` outpoint spent by its `0x09` transaction (`ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` on mismatch);
   - `seallink-end/1` `issuer` MUST equal `origin.end_authority` (or `origin.capacity_authority` if `end_authority` is omitted, or `origin.issuer` if both are omitted), and its `status_prev_outpoint` MUST equal the exact current `status_seal` outpoint spent by its `0x09` transaction (`ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` on mismatch);
   - `seallink-guardian/1` and `seallink-guardian-roster/1` `issuer` MUST equal `origin.issuer` (or `origin.capacity_authority` when present).
   Any verifier checking current status walks the status spine from `origin.status_seal` on its own node (with bounded walk depth of `256` transitions / `<= 257` seals, failing closed with `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` if exhausted), verifying signatures against the authorized issuer's `link.cred` policy resolved at anchor height (bounded by `0x0b` `h_c`):

| Current Spine State | Observed Spend of Current `status_seal` | Next Spine State | Verifier Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `initial` | Unspent at tip and no valid spend in mempool. | `initial` (`ward` if `guardian_policy` present, else `autonomous`) | Continue verification. |
| `initial` | Confirmed `0x09` (`>= required_depth`) matching presented `seallink-capacity/1` (exact authority `NamePin` verified at anchor height) creating `next_status_policy` at `next_status_vout`. | `emancipated` (successor `status_seal = (T_cap, next_status_vout)`) | Continue walking from `(T_cap, next_status_vout)`; require confirmed handover transfer (`SL-ORIG-011`). |
| `initial` or `emancipated` | Confirmed `0x09` (`>= required_depth`) matching presented `seallink-end/1` (exact authority `NamePin` verified at anchor height). | `ended` (terminal; no successor seal) | Durably close all guardianship slots and refuse `SUBJECT_ENDED`. |
| `initial` or `emancipated` | Spent in mempool or block (or `absent_from_utxo`) without matching presented `capacity/1` or `end/1`, or malformed `next_status_vout` / unauthorized issuer. | `incomplete` | Fail closed with `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE`. |

7. **Eternal validity & compromise cutoff:** Verified against `issuer.role_policy` (`link.cred`) **at `h_origin`**, surviving routine key rotation and name expiry, provided `h_origin < h_c` under any `0x0b` compromise break (`CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`). Issuer services MUST enforce durable idempotency by `request_sha256`.

### 2.2 Single-Use Name Binding and History-Based Origin Detection (`SL-ORIG-003`, `decided`)

1. `seallink-origin/1` names a pre-created confirmed single-use `binding_seal` matching its compiled `ScriptPolicy`.
2. After `OP_NAME_FIRSTUPDATE` for `registration` confirms at `h_firstupdate > h_origin` with trailing output script matching `claims.intended_owner_policy`, the registrar spends `binding_seal` (`SIGHASH_ALL`) in a confirmed `OP_RETURN 0x0c` transaction (`>= required_depth`) committing `id(seallink-name-binding/1)` with `seq = 1`, `origin_id = id(origin/1)`, `network`/`genesis` matching local pin, `name == claims.intended_name`, `owner_policy == claims.intended_owner_policy`, and active `registration` (`ORIGIN_BINDING_INVALID` on any mismatch or unspent/malformed `binding_seal`). Verifiers MUST verify that `binding.registration` exists on the active chain and MUST NEVER fall back to a different registration of the same name spelling (`NAME_NOT_FOUND` / `ORIGIN_BINDING_INVALID`).
3. **Mandatory `link.origin` on `0x0c` `OP_NAME_FIRSTUPDATE` & History-Based Permanent Origin Detection:** For EVERY `0x0c`-bound registration (`binding_mode ∈ {"initial", "recovery"}`, NOT affecting `0x13 VOLUNTARY_REBIND` targets which write `link.origin` only at Step 3 activation `h_act >= h_13 + D`), `OP_NAME_FIRSTUPDATE` (`updates[0]`) **MUST** commit `{"link":{"origin":"<origin_id_64hex>"}}` (refusing **`ORIGIN_BINDING_INVALID`** if absent or mismatched) so cold observers inspecting `name_history` immediately discover ward status from block 0 of the registration without any blind window. Verifiers MUST scan the entire confirmed update history of `registration` (from `OP_NAME_FIRSTUPDATE` through tip) for any `link.origin` pin AND check any presented or observed `0x0c` name binding for that registration — NEVER relying solely on the tip name value. Removing or altering `link.origin` in a later update NEVER unbinds an already confirmed registration and MUST NOT allow a ward to bypass guardianship by erasing `link.origin` (`WARD_AUTONOMOUS_FORBIDDEN` / `ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED`).
4. **Reverse uniqueness:** A registration already bound to one `origin_id` MUST refuse any second initial binding (`ORIGIN_BINDING_INVALID`).

## 3. Multi-Guardian Slots, Closed Event Classes, and Slot Transitions

### 3.1 Revision-Denominated Quorums and Verifier-Derived `event_class` (`SL-ORIG-004`, `SL-ORIG-005`, `decided`)

1. `origin.guardian_policy` initializes `revision = 1`, `revision_size = N` (`1 <= N <= 32`), explicit **`BranchKeySpec`** institutional signers **`disposer_pk`**, independent **`arbiter_pk`** (`arbiter_pk != disposer_pk`, disjoint key sets), and pre-activation **`challenger_pk`** (each either a single `CompressedPubkey66` or a compact native `P2WSH` `m-of-n` multisig with `2 <= n <= 5`, `SPEC-02 §3.1`), `roster_seal` (`both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)`), `appointment_delay_blocks` (`1..65535`, matching the BIP-68 16-bit CSV maximum), a closed 4-class `QuorumMap`, and `slots[1..N]` (each beginning in `initial_vacant` with `vacant_utxo` locked by `both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)`).
2. **Closed 4-class linear `GuardianEventClass` (verifier-derived — NEVER signer-chosen):** ONLY objects carrying `payload.guardian_context` (`seallink-grant/1`, `seallink-assent/1`, `seallink-termination/1`, and `seallink-statement/1` with `statement_kind = "identity-link"`) map to `GuardianEventClass`. The four classes form a strict linear order `routine_read < effect_grant < contract_assent < custody_change`. The verifier deterministically derives the required class from the object type, structural flags (`delegate`, `contract_id`, `single_use`, `quotas`), and each capability's `action` via `registry/actions.json` (single source of truth, taking the maximum across all capabilities; any unrecognized/custom action NOT in `registry/actions.json` **MUST fail closed to `contract_assent`** — or `custody_change` when `delegate` is present — and MUST NEVER downgrade to `routine_read`), and MUST verify `payload.guardian_context.event_class == derived_class` (`GUARDIAN_QUORUM_UNMET` on mismatch):

| `GuardianEventClass` | Normative Carrier Objects & Scope (Highest Matching Class Applies) |
| --- | --- |
| `routine_read` | Stateless read-only `seallink-grant/1` whose every capability `action` is registered in `registry/actions.json` with `guardian_event_class = "routine_read"` (`external_mutation = false`, no `quotas`, no `delegate`, not `single_use`, no `contract_id`). |
| `effect_grant` | Unilateral `seallink-grant/1` (no `delegate`, no `contract_id`) whose capability carries `quotas`, `single_use` revocation, OR an action registered in `registry/actions.json` with `guardian_event_class = "effect_grant"` (such as `mail/send`, `agent/tool`, `agent/guardian-message`). |
| `contract_assent` | `seallink-assent/1` with `target_type = "seallink-contract/1"`, `seallink-termination/1` executed on behalf of a ward party (`SPEC-05 §2.3`), `seallink-statement/1` with `statement_kind = "identity-link"` linking a ward into an organization or controller relationship (`SPEC-03 §3.1`), any contract-bound `seallink-grant/1` (`contract_id` present, without `delegate`), OR any grant containing an action registered with `guardian_event_class = "contract_assent"` or an unknown/custom action (fail-closed default). |
| `custody_change` | Delegating `seallink-grant/1` (`delegate` present), `seallink-assent/1` with `target_type = "seallink-migration/1"`, OR any grant containing an action registered with `guardian_event_class = "custody_change"`. |

   *(Roster transitions `0x0f`, emancipation `seallink-capacity/1`, subject end `seallink-end/1`, and dual-party name recovery `0x0c` are governed directly by their dedicated on-chain seals and `issuer.role_policy` — never by `guardian_approvals[]` — so a deadlocked or burned guardian quorum never blocks institutional governance.)*
3. **Revision-denominated threshold (`SL-ORIG-005`):** For derived class `E` with quorum `{num, den, min}` from the active roster revision, required distinct valid guardian slots MUST be computed strictly against **`revision_size`** (NEVER against transient active slot count):
   ```text
   required(E) = max(min, ceil(num * revision_size / den))
   ```
4. **Distinct guardian rule:** Only an `autonomous` actor MAY hold a guardianship slot. Two slots MAY NOT simultaneously be held by the same `(name, registration)`. Each approving slot supplies its own independent `SignatureSet` inside `guardian_approvals[]` matching that guardian's resolved `link.grant` policy (`SL-WIRE-013`), so a multi-key guardian satisfies its own threshold independently and counts as at most one slot.

### 3.2 Guardianship Consistency Invariants (`SL-ORIG-016`, `decided`)

Every verifier validating `seallink-origin/1`, `seallink-guardian-roster/1`, or `seallink-guardian/1` MUST enforce these mechanical invariants:

1. **Rational quorum bounds & monotonicity:** For every entry `{num, den, min}` in `quorums`, `1 <= num <= den <= 32` and `1 <= min <= revision_size` MUST hold, AND derived thresholds MUST be monotonically non-decreasing across the linear class order: `required(routine_read) <= required(effect_grant) <= required(contract_assent) <= required(custody_change)` (`WIRE_INTEGER` on violation). *(Note: signed ward envelopes remain subject to per-type `max_bytes` — `65536`B for `seallink-grant/1` and `32768`B for `seallink-assent/1` — so deployments combining high `revision_size` with large per-guardian `ThresholdPolicy` sets MUST budget envelope size accordingly.)*
2. **Exact slot coverage & distinct outpoints:** `len(slots) == revision_size`, all `slot` numbers MUST be distinct, and the set of slot numbers MUST equal `{1, ..., revision_size}` (`WIRE_INTEGER` on violation). All seal outpoints declared across an origin certificate (`binding_seal`, `status_seal`, `roster_seal`, `recovery_seal`, and every `slots[i].vacant_utxo`) MUST be pairwise distinct (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` on duplicate outpoint).
3. **Key independence, script binding, and delay floor:** `disposer_pk != arbiter_pk` (distinct canonical `BranchKeySpec` and disjoint compressed key sets) MUST hold on BOTH `guardian_policy` and `name_recovery` (`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED` on violation). `roster_seal.script_policy`, `next_roster_policy`, and every `vacant_utxo` / `new_vacant_policy` MUST compile to `both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)`. Whenever a `seallink-guardian-roster/1` transition changes `disposer_pk` or `arbiter_pk` relative to the previous roster revision, `retain_active` and `retain_vacant` are **FORBIDDEN** — every slot in the new revision MUST use `new_vacant` locked to `both_of_2(new_disposer_pk, new_arbiter_pk)` (`CHAIN_SCRIPT_UNSUPPORTED` on violation). Every `seallink-guardian-roster/1` MUST satisfy `effective_delay_blocks >= origin.guardian_policy.appointment_delay_blocks` (`GUARDIAN_INACTIVE` / `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` on shortfall), and no `0x0d` appointment spending a `new_vacant` output from a `0x0f` transition is valid before the roster takes effect (`h_app >= h_roster + effective_delay_blocks`, else `GUARDIAN_INACTIVE`). Every Stage 1 `pending_policy` MUST compile to `guardian_slot_pending` with `disposer == active_roster.disposer_pk`, `challenger == active_roster.challenger_pk`, `guardian == guardian_seal_pk`, and `delay_blocks == effective_delay_blocks >= origin.guardian_policy.appointment_delay_blocks` (`CHAIN_SCRIPT_UNSUPPORTED` on script/key mismatch; `GUARDIAN_INACTIVE` on delay shortfall). Every `active_policy` MUST compile to `either_of_2(guardian_seal_pk, active_roster.disposer_pk)`.
4. **Term ceiling against majority:** For a human ward (`origin_class = "person"`) whose `claims.majority_commitment` is opened to `{"majority_unix": T}`, every `seallink-guardian/1` carrying a `calendar` term MUST satisfy `term.not_after_unix <= T` (`GUARDIAN_INACTIVE` on violation).

### 3.3 Protocol-Level Ward Non-Autonomy (`SL-ORIG-006`, `decided`)

While a subject is in `ward` status (verified `origin/1` with `guardian_policy` present and no completed emancipation), every verifier evaluating a `seallink-grant/1`, `seallink-assent/1`, `seallink-termination/1`, or `seallink-statement/1` (`statement_kind = "identity-link"`) for that subject MUST enforce this single canonical refusal precedence:

1. Missing `payload.guardian_context` or carrying top-level `signatures` instead of `guardian_approvals[]` -> **`WARD_AUTONOMOUS_FORBIDDEN`**
2. `payload.guardian_context.origin_id` or `ward_name` mismatch -> **`ORIGIN_UNVERIFIED`**
3. `payload.guardian_context.revision` mismatch with latest effective roster revision, OR any `guardian_approvals[i].revision != payload.guardian_context.revision` -> **`GUARDIAN_QUORUM_UNMET`**
4. **Pass 1 — All slot states before any signature or `0x0b` checks:** Across ALL entries in `guardian_approvals[]` (strictly ascending `slot`, checked at wire step -> `WIRE_SIGNATURE`), verify before testing any entry's cryptographic signature or `0x0b` break status that: slot is in the evaluated roster revision, slot is not durably closed (`guardian_slot` closure in journal), slot state == `active`, active guardian name == `entry.guardian_name`, calendar/ceiling term is unexpired at evaluation time/height, approving guardian's own `(name, registration, owner_policy)` is live and passes writer-verified history (including `link.grant` continuity back to `recent_block` for live grants, preventing `A -> B -> A` rotation rollback), and all approving slots resolve to pairwise-distinct `(name, registration)` guardians -> **`GUARDIAN_INACTIVE`**.
5. **Pass 2 — Guardian signatures & `0x0b` compromise check (historical AND live tip):** For every entry, verify `entry.signatures.policy` strictly equals that guardian's resolved `link.grant` policy (at tip for live grants, or at `h_anchor` for anchored historical contracts), check retroactive `0x0b` `CredentialBreak` for `role = "grant"` at the evaluation height (`h_eval >= h_c` refuses **`CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`**, while historical `h_anchor < h_c` survives; Pass-1 `GUARDIAN_INACTIVE` always takes precedence over Pass-2 `CRED_ISSUER_CLOSED`), and verify BIP-340 signatures -> **`WIRE_SIGNATURE`**.
6. `payload.guardian_context.event_class != derived_class`, OR count of distinct satisfied slots `< required(derived_class)` -> **`GUARDIAN_QUORUM_UNMET`**.

**Intentional fail-safe halt on live grants vs. survival of concluded contracts:** Live ward `seallink-grant/1` objects are evaluated against the **latest effective roster revision** and each approving guardian's **live `link.grant` policy at tip** (requiring `recent_block`-to-tip continuity). Consequently, any effective `0x0f` roster transition or rotation of an approving guardian's `link.grant` immediately halts prior operational ward grants (`GUARDIAN_QUORUM_UNMET` or `WIRE_SIGNATURE`) and requires the new roster / updated keys to reissue them. By contrast, an **already-anchored `seallink-contract/1`** evaluates each ward party's `seallink-assent/1` against the ward's active roster, active slots, and approving guardians' `link.grant` policies **at verified contract anchor height `h_anchor`** (bounded by retroactive `0x0b` `h_c`), so routine guardian rotations or `0x0f` roster transitions never invalidate already-concluded contracts (`SPEC-05 §2.2`).

### 3.4 Initial Activation (`0x12`), Acyclic Two-Stage Appointment (`0x0d`/`0x0e`), and Slot State Machine (`SL-ORIG-007`, `decided`)

To eliminate `txid <-> hash` cycles, preserve privacy of initial guardians, and eliminate indefinite challenger vetoes, slot activation supports two explicit modes in `seallink-guardian/1` (`activation_mode`):

1. **Initial Slot Activation (`activation_mode = "initial"`, `0x12`):**
   - Each slot declared in `origin.guardian_policy.slots[i]` begins in state **`initial_vacant`** at `vacant_utxo` (`both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)`).
   - To activate initial guardian `G_init` without delay: the presenter opens `(salt_i, initial_guardian_NamePin)` verifying `commit(salt_i, initial_guardian_NamePin) == origin.guardian_policy.slots[i].initial_guardian_commitment` (`SL-WIRE-014`); `G_init` signs `seallink-assent/1` (`target_type = "seallink-guardian/1"`); and `issuer` signs `seallink-guardian/1` with `activation_mode = "initial"`, `revision = 1`, `prev_outpoint = vacant_utxo`, `active_vout`, and `active_policy = either_of_2(guardian_seal_pk, disposer_pk)`.
   - Both `disposer_pk` and `arbiter_pk` co-sign one PSBT spending `vacant_utxo` (`both_of_2`), creating `active_policy` at `active_vout`, and anchoring `content_addr(seallink-guardian/1)` in `OP_RETURN 0x12` (`GUARDIAN_INITIAL_ACTIVATION`).
   - Once confirmed at `h_init` (`>= required_depth`) with txid `T_init` (verifying `prev_outpoint == slot.vacant_utxo` and `T_init` spends `prev_outpoint`), the live active slot seal is deterministically **`(T_init, active_vout)`**. `activation_mode = "initial"` is permitted ONLY from `initial_vacant` at `revision = 1`; appointing ANY successor MUST use `activation_mode = "appointed"` below.
2. **Stage 1 — Successor Appointment (`activation_mode = "appointed"`, `0x0d`, creates `pending_delay`):**
   - Incoming guardian `G_new` signs `seallink-assent/1` (`target_type = "seallink-guardian/1"`).
   - Appointing authority (`issuer.role_policy`, verified against `link.cred` at `h_app` with exact authority `NamePin` match) signs the **acyclic** `seallink-guardian/1` with `activation_mode = "appointed"`, committing `prev_outpoint` (MUST strictly equal the slot's current `vacant` or `initial_vacant` outpoint), `pending_vout`, `pending_policy` (`guardian_slot_pending` with `{disposer: active_roster.disposer_pk, challenger: active_roster.challenger_pk, guardian: guardian_seal_pk}` and `delay_blocks = effective_delay_blocks`), and `active_policy` (`either_of_2(guardian_seal_pk, active_roster.disposer_pk)`).
   - Both `disposer_pk` and `arbiter_pk` co-sign one PSBT spending `prev_outpoint` (`both_of_2`), creating `pending_policy` at `pending_vout`, and anchoring `content_addr(seallink-guardian/1)` in `OP_RETURN 0x0d`. If `prev_outpoint` originated as a `new_vacant` output of a `0x0f` roster transition at `h_roster`, `h_app >= h_roster + effective_delay_blocks` MUST hold (`GUARDIAN_INACTIVE` if premature).
   - Once confirmed at `h_app` (`>= required_depth`) with txid `T_app`, the pending slot outpoint is deterministically **`(T_app, pending_vout)`**. During `[h_app, h_app + delay_blocks)`, the slot has zero authority; `disposer_pk` or `challenger_pk` MAY spend `(T_app, pending_vout)` to veto the appointment.
3. **Stage 2 — Activation (`0x0e`, removes challenger veto permanently):**
   - At height `h_act >= h_app + delay_blocks`, `G_new` spends `(T_app, pending_vout)` via the CSV-unlocked `<guardian>` branch (`SIGHASH_ALL`, BIP-68 input `sequence` with bit 31 and bit 22 cleared and `(sequence & 0xffff) >= delay_blocks`, and exact 4-item witness `[sig_guardian, "", "", witnessScript]` where `wit[-1]` equals `compile(pending_policy)` witnessScript and BOTH `OP_IF` selectors evaluate FALSE under Bitcoin Script `CastToBool` — rejecting disposer or challenger veto spends that masquerade as `0x0e`), creating `active_policy` (`either_of_2(guardian_seal_pk, active_roster.disposer_pk)`) at output `vout = 0`, and anchoring `id(seallink-guardian/1)` in `OP_RETURN 0x0e`.
   - Once confirmed at `h_act` (`>= required_depth`) with txid `T_act`, the live active slot seal is deterministically **`(T_act, 0)`**. `challenger_pk` is absent from `active_policy` and has zero power once activated.
4. **Fast Halt (1 party, mempool speed), Unconfirmed Non-Burning, & Journaled Re-Appointment (`D14-H05`):** Either `guardian` resigns OR `disposer_pk` halts immediately by spending the active slot seal (`either_of_2`) to a fresh `vacant` UTXO locked by `both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)` (optionally carrying `0x0a` for-cause withdrawal, which bars re-appointment without a new ruling). First observation in mempool permanently terminates that guardian's authority (`GUARDIAN_INACTIVE` on ward actions; `SEAL_SPENT` on direct seal check). When a confirmed spend cleanly transitions `active -> vacant` (recreating `both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)` at `vout = 0`), verifiers MUST advance the slot walk to the new `vacant` UTXO WITHOUT recording a `contested_burned` closure on the old active outpoint (only non-`vacant` burns or unconfirmed mempool halts record permanent outpoint closure), so a stateful journaled verifier cleanly accepts subsequent re-appointment (`active -> vacant -> 0x0d -> 0x0e -> active`) on the same slot! Conversely, unconfirmed `0x12`/`0x0d`/`0x0e`/`0x0f` transition spends MUST NOT transition a vacant/pending slot into `contested_burned` (they are ignored until confirmed). Slot and roster walk bounds are `256` transitions (`<= 257` seals); exhausting the walk bound MUST return `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` (never `contested_burned`).

#### Complete Slot State Transition Table

Because Namecoin lacks output covenants, a malicious spender could spend a slot UTXO to an invalid output script ("burning" the slot). Every possible spend is handled deterministically:

| Current State | Observed Spend / Event | Next State | Guardian Authority | Recovery Path |
| --- | --- | --- | :-: | --- |
| `initial_vacant` | Valid `0x12` tx (`both_of_2`) for disclosed `initial_guardian_commitment` + guardian assent creating `active_policy` at `active_vout`. | `active` (outpoint `(T_init, active_vout)`) | **Active** | Normal operation. |
| `initial_vacant` | Valid `0x0d` tx (`both_of_2`, `activation_mode = "appointed"`) creating `pending_policy` at `pending_vout`. | `pending_delay` (outpoint `(T_app, pending_vout)`) | None | Stage 2 after `delay_blocks`. |

### 3.5 Roster Revision Transitions (`seallink-guardian-roster/1`, `0x0f`, `SL-ORIG-015`, `decided`)

To change approved `revision_size` (e.g., reducing `2 -> 1` when one parent loses parental rights, adding a slot, rotating `disposer_pk`/`arbiter_pk`/`challenger_pk`, updating `quorums`, or re-anchoring a `contested_burned` slot):

1. **Authorization without old-roster quorum:** Roster transitions are authorized strictly by `disposer_pk + arbiter_pk` co-signing a PSBT spending the current `roster_seal` (`both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)`), combined with `issuer.role_policy` (`link.cred`) signing `seallink-guardian-roster/1` and waiting `effective_delay_blocks`. They NEVER require signatures from the old guardian roster, ensuring deadlocked or burned rosters remain recoverable.
2. The `0x0f` transaction creates `next_roster_policy` (`both_of_2(new_disposer_pk, new_arbiter_pk)`) at `next_roster_vout`, creates any `new_vacant` slot UTXOs at their declared `new_vacant_vout` indices, and anchors `content_addr(seallink-guardian-roster/1)` via `OP_RETURN 0x0f`. Each slot entry in `slots[]` specifies one of three transition actions (each carried-forward outpoint from the immediately preceding revision MAY be referenced at most once, allowing deterministic slot renumbering when shrinking `revision_size`):
   - **`retain_active`:** Carries forward an unspent active slot outpoint `(active_outpoint, guardian_id)` that existed as an active slot in the immediately preceding revision (FORBIDDEN if `disposer_pk` or `arbiter_pk` changed, because the existing `either_of_2` output would still reference the retired `disposer_pk`).
   - **`retain_vacant`:** Carries forward an unspent vacant slot outpoint (`vacant_outpoint`) that existed as a vacant or initial-vacant slot in the immediately preceding revision (never an arbitrary UTXO; FORBIDDEN if `disposer_pk` or `arbiter_pk` changed, because the existing `both_of_2` output would still reference retired keys).
   - **`new_vacant`:** Creates a fresh `both_of_2(new_disposer_pk, new_arbiter_pk)` vacant slot UTXO at `(txid_0x0f, new_vacant_vout)` (REQUIRED for every slot whenever `disposer_pk` or `arbiter_pk` changes, and used to recover `contested_burned` slots or add new slots). **Operational consequence:** because rotating `disposer_pk` or `arbiter_pk` recreates all slots as `new_vacant`, the ward remains without active guardians (fail-closed) for at least one appointment delay window (`appointment_delay_blocks`) until successor appointments complete `0x0d -> 0x0e`.
3. `payload.revision` MUST equal `prev_revision + 1`, `payload.prev_roster_outpoint` MUST equal the spent previous roster outpoint, and `payload` MUST satisfy all `SL-ORIG-016` invariants (`len(slots) == revision_size`, `1 <= num <= den`, `1 <= min <= revision_size`, monotonic `QuorumMap`, `disposer_pk != arbiter_pk`, `effective_delay_blocks >= origin.guardian_policy.appointment_delay_blocks`, and no `retain_active` / `retain_vacant` when `disposer_pk` or `arbiter_pk` changes -> `CHAIN_SCRIPT_UNSUPPORTED`).
4. The new roster takes effect after `effective_delay_blocks` confirmations (`roster_eff_h = h_roster + effective_delay_blocks`, requiring `effective_delay_blocks >= origin.guardian_policy.appointment_delay_blocks`). When evaluating a transition from Revision `R` to Revision `R + 1`, verifiers MUST trace Revision `R`'s slots strictly up to `roster_eff_h` (NOT `eval_h`) to verify `retain_active` / `retain_vacant` carry-over eligibility at `roster_eff_h`, and then trace all spends occurring after `roster_eff_h` strictly inside Revision `R + 1` (preventing post-transition spends of a legitimately carried-over slot from falsely burning the slot in Revision `R`). While `roster_seal` is spent and `seallink-guardian-roster/1` is not presented (or fails verification, or walk depth exceeds `256`), verifiers fail closed with `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE`.
5. **Issuer-key survival (`SL-ORIG-008`, `decided`):** Both `seallink-guardian/1` and `seallink-guardian-roster/1` verify `issuer.role_policy` (`link.cred`) **at their confirmed anchor height**, bounded by `0x0b` (`h_c`), so routine issuer key rotation never orphans wards.

## 4. Guardianship Terms: Indefinite vs. Calendar Time

| Subject / Mode | `term` Form | Termination Rule (`SL-ORIG-009`, `decided`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Agent / Indefinite** | `{"mode":"indefinite"}` | Lives until slot seal spend, verified `seallink-capacity/1`, verified `seallink-end/1`, or compromise cutoff `h_c`. |
| **Human Minor / Calendar** | `{"mode":"calendar","not_after_unix":T,"ceiling_height":H_max}` | Guardianship ends automatically at calendar majority `T` (bounded by `T <= opened majority_unix` per `SL-ORIG-016`, and never exceeding `H_max`, which is a failsafe ceiling and never extends past `T`). |

**Asymmetric Safety Rule at Majority Boundary (`SL-ORIG-010`, `open` time-interval oracle profile; safety rule `decided`):**
Block intervals drift by weeks over 18 years; chain median time past (MTP, `tip_median_time_past`) is a **MANDATORY** auxiliary necessary constraint (`tip_median_time_past < T` MUST hold for guardian admission; omitting `tip_median_time_past` from `ChainView` when evaluating a ward with `majority_commitment` MUST fail closed with `GUARDIAN_TIME_BOUNDARY`), never sufficient alone.
- Guardian admission is allowed ONLY if the entire credible UTC interval `[t_low, t_high]` lies strictly below `T`, `tip_median_time_past < T`, `claims.majority_commitment` has been opened to `T`, and tip is fresh under `SPEC-02`.
- Once `t_low >= T` for a `person`, guardian authority ceases automatically and unconditionally without requiring any institution (`State 3: GUARDIAN_TRANSFER_PENDING`, immediately revoking former-guardian `0x10` recovery-cancel veto). Advancing to `State 4 (autonomous)` requires cryptographically authenticated emancipation handover or `0x0c` recovery (`SL-ORIG-011`).
- If `[t_low, t_high]` straddles `T`, `tip_median_time_past` is missing or `>= T` while `t_low < T`, tip is stale, or the credible interval cannot be established, admission pauses for BOTH sides (`GUARDIAN_TIME_BOUNDARY`). *(The normative trusted time-interval oracle profile is tracked in `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`.)*

## 5. Ward Admission State Machine, Unified Emancipation, and Subject End

### 5.1 Four Ward Admission States and Unified Authenticated Emancipation (`SL-ORIG-011`, `decided`)

A verifier evaluating any action for an origin-certified actor walks `origin/1`, `status_seal`, `roster_seal`, `binding_seal`, and active slot seals:

| State | Condition | Permitted Admission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Under Guardianship** | `status_seal` unspent (no `capacity/1` or `end/1`); for `person`, `majority_commitment` opened and `t_high < T` and `tip_median_time_past < T`; active slot(s) at latest effective roster `revision` satisfy `required(derived_event_class)` via `guardian_approvals[]`; confirmed name binding matches current `owner_policy`. | Actions authorized by guardian quorum (and separate service grant where required). |
| **2. Guardianship Halted** | Not in State 3/4, but active slots cannot meet `required(derived_event_class)` (halted, vacant, pending, or `contested_burned`). | None (`GUARDIAN_INACTIVE` if cited slot is inactive; `GUARDIAN_QUORUM_UNMET` if valid active slots fall below `required`). |
| **3. Transfer Pending** | Emancipation trigger reached — either **(a)** calendar majority `t_low >= T` for `person` (automatic, institution-free guardian cutoff), OR **(b)** confirmed `seallink-capacity/1` spent `status_seal` naming `subject_new_owner_policy` (`person` or `agent`) — BEFORE authenticated handover or `0x0c` recovery completes. | None for either side (`GUARDIAN_TRANSFER_PENDING`). Name renewal, revocation, handover transfer, and `dual_party_delayed` recovery (`0x0c`) remain allowed; **former guardians lose `0x10` recovery-cancel veto immediately upon entering State 3** (evaluated at the `0x10` transaction's own block time/height) so a hostile former guardian cannot block handover recovery. |
| **4. Autonomous** | Unified for BOTH `person` and `agent` — requires cryptographically authenticated subject key ownership via EITHER:<br>1. **Cooperative Handover (`capacity/1`):** Confirmed `seallink-capacity/1` spending `status_seal` naming `subject_new_owner_policy` + confirmed `NAME_UPDATE` at `u_xfer >= h_capacity_anchor` transferring the active binding to `subject_new_owner_policy` + confirmed subject-authored `SIGHASH_ALL` `OP_NAME_UPDATE` under `subject_new_owner_policy`; **OR**<br>2. **Governed Recovery (`0x0c` taking effect at/after State 3):** Confirmed `dual_party_delayed` `0x0c` recovery (`seq >= 2`, delay elapsed with no valid `0x10`) binding `origin_id` to a fresh subject registration + confirmed subject-authored `SIGHASH_ALL` self-update, satisfying **strict post-trigger promotion timing**: EITHER `h_rec + delay_blocks >= h_capacity_anchor` (for `capacity/1`, allowing key recovery to fresh `K2 != K1` if `subject_new_owner_policy` `K1` was lost) OR BIP-113 `block_times[h_rec + delay_blocks] >= T` (for calendar majority `T`; a childhood `0x0c` promoted before `T` MUST NEVER auto-emancipate when `T` arrives and MUST remain `GUARDIAN_TRANSFER_PENDING`).<br>**Mandatory pubkey disjointness (`M-9`):** In BOTH paths, the emancipated `owner_policy` MUST share **zero** secp256k1 **32-byte x-coordinates** (matching `ScriptPolicy` disjointness in `SL-LDGR-004`, rejecting `02X`/`03X` negated-point reuse) with `intended_owner_policy` or any pre-trigger guardian update (treating missing `block_time` on pre-trigger updates fail-closed as pre-majority) or childhood `0x0c` owner policy (`GUARDIAN_TRANSFER_PENDING` if any x-coordinate overlaps). Cold observers MUST resolve `block_times[h_rec + delay_blocks]` using strictly verified BIP-113 `mediantime` (never substituting ordinary `header.time`) inside the same observation bracket for every maturity height `<= tip_height`. | Full autonomous protocol admission; guardianship slots permanently `closed`. **Effective active owner policy advances to `effective_binding.owner_policy`** (the emancipated subject's `owner_policy`), enabling autonomous grants, contracts, and routine owner key rotation. |

- **Why State 4 always requires authenticated subject keys:** Calendar majority `T` alone transitions a human ward into State 3 (`GUARDIAN_TRANSFER_PENDING`) as an automatic institution-free safety cutoff, preventing former guardians from acting or blocking recovery. Requiring `seallink-capacity/1` (`subject_new_owner_policy`) or governed `0x0c` recovery to enter State 4 prevents colluding custodians from faking a self-transfer (`A -> B`) to impersonate the newly adult subject, while providing one unified autonomy mechanism for both humans and autonomous agents.
- **Subject End (`SL-ORIG-012`, `decided`):** An eternal `seallink-end/1` record spending `status_seal` terminally (`0x09`, `status_prev_outpoint` matching spent seal, creating no successor `status_seal`) permanently closes all guardianship slots and refuses all future admission (`SUBJECT_ENDED`). Silence or lost keys NEVER constitute proof of end.

## 6. Dual-Mode Name Recovery (`name_recovery`) and Seal Continuation

`seallink-origin/1` immutably declares one of two recovery policies at issuance (`SL-ORIG-013`, `decided`):

1. **`"mode": "none"` (Irreversible Binding — autonomous/organization origins ONLY):** FORBIDDEN whenever `guardian_policy` is present (`WIRE_MEMBER`). No third party can ever re-bind `origin_id` to a new ledger name. Attempting recovery refuses `RECOVERY_FORBIDDEN`.
2. **`"mode": "dual_party_delayed"` (Governed Recovery):**
   - `origin/1` pins `disposer_pk`, independent `arbiter_pk` (`BranchKeySpec`, `disposer_pk != arbiter_pk`), `delay_blocks ∈ [144, 525600]` (recommended `>= 1008`; note `0x13 VOLUNTARY_REBIND` keeps `delay_blocks <= 35000` under Namecoin Binding Profile v1 `D = 36000`), and a `both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)` `recovery_seal`.
   - To recover after catastrophic key loss (or refusal by former guardians to execute a Path A / Path B emancipation handover), both `disposer_pk` and `arbiter_pk` co-sign a Witness Profile `W` (`SIGHASH_ALL`) transaction `T_rec` spending the live `recovery_seal` with `OP_RETURN 0x0c` committing a new `seallink-name-binding/1` (`seq = prev_binding.seq + 1`, naming the new confirmed registration, new `owner_policy`, `supersedes_binding_id = id(prev_binding)` [or the stalled unknown-preimage `0x13` commitment during `unknown_rebind_stalled` recovery], AND continuation `next_recovery_vout` + `next_recovery_policy = both_of_2(disposer_pk, arbiter_pk)`). **Mandatory complete target lineage (`D14-B10`):** before opening any `0x0c` proposal (`_try_open_0x0c`), verifiers MUST verify that the target registration exists with complete, non-truncated confirmed history in `ChainView` (`ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` if missing or truncated — NEVER skipping verification!), that `updates[0].op == "NAME_FIRSTUPDATE"` confirmed at `h_firstupdate <= h_rec`, that its trailing script matches `binding.owner_policy` (and `claims.intended_owner_policy` on `seq == 1`), and that `updates[0]` commits `link.origin == origin_id` (`ORIGIN_BINDING_INVALID` on violation). Any `binding_seal` or `recovery_seal` spend violating Witness Profile `W` refuses `CHAIN_TX_CONSTRUCTION`.
   - Once `T_rec` confirms at `h_rec`, the live recovery seal advances to `(T_rec, next_recovery_vout)`. During `[h_rec, h_rec + delay_blocks)`, the previous binding stops admitting and the new binding sits in `pending_delay` (`RECOVERY_FORBIDDEN` for both bindings, enforced consistently for BOTH live tip evaluation and historical `at_height` evaluation whenever `h_rec <= eval_h < h_rec + delay_blocks` without a valid `0x10` cancel).
   - **Live-owner cancellation veto (`SL-ORIG-014`, `decided`):** At any block strictly prior to `h_rec + delay_blocks` (and `h_cancel <= eval_h` for historical evaluation) — PROVIDED the origin was NOT in State 3 (`transfer_pending`) **at `(h_cancel, cancel_block_time)` itself** (non-retroactive lockout: when `claims.majority_commitment` is opened to `T`, `cancel_block_time` [BIP-113 `mediantime`] MUST be present and strictly `< T`, failing closed as locked out if omitted; and `h_cancel < h_capacity_anchor` for any confirmed `capacity/1`, UNLESS the origin had already completed authenticated State-4 emancipation strictly prior to `h_cancel`; **rotating guardian key `W1 -> W2` prior to `T`/`h_cap` MUST NEVER bypass State-3 lockout**, while an already-emancipated State-4 adult MAY cancel a subsequent rogue `0x0c`; a valid `0x10` veto confirmed before lockout is NEVER retroactively invalidated after `T` or later emancipation) — the live binding `B_k`'s exact `(name, registration, owner_policy)` MAY broadcast an owner-authored (`SIGHASH_ALL`) `OP_NAME_UPDATE` containing `OP_RETURN 0x10` (`RECOVERY_CANCEL`) committing `id(pending_name_binding/1)`. Verifiers MUST scan the entire confirmed update history of `B_k.registration` during `[h_rec, h_rec + delay_blocks)` (never an unrelated re-registration of the same spelling, and never losing a confirmed `0x10` after subsequent routine owner key rotation or expiry). Observing confirmed `0x10` cryptographically proves the old keys are alive, permanently aborts the pending recovery (`RECOVERY_FORBIDDEN` for the cancelled binding), **immediately restores admission under `B_k`**, and preserves the continued `recovery_seal` at `(T_rec, next_recovery_vout)` for any genuine future recovery.
   - Old grants bound to the old name/key NEVER carry over to a recovered name.

### 6.1 Voluntary Name Rebinding (`0x13 VOLUNTARY_REBIND`) and 5-Subphase Deterministic Automaton (`SL-ORIG-017`, `decided`)

An **autonomous (`State 4`)** origin-certified subject (under EITHER `name_recovery.mode = "dual_party_delayed"` OR `"none"`) MAY voluntarily migrate its active `origin_id` binding from current binding `B_1` to a clean new registration `B_2` (`binding_mode = "voluntary"`, `seq = prev + 1`) using unmodified Namecoin Core `nc28.0` (`nVersion = 0x7100`, max 1 name-input and max 1 name-output per transaction):

1. **Discriminated `seallink-name-binding/1` (`schemas/guardian.schema.json`):** Every `seallink-name-binding/1` carries a mandatory `binding_mode` discriminator: `"initial"` (`seq == 1`, `0x0c` via `binding_seal`), `"recovery"` (`seq >= 2`, `0x0c` via `recovery_seal` with `next_recovery_vout`/`next_recovery_policy`), or `"voluntary"` (`seq >= 2`, `0x13` with `consent_outpoint` and `delay_blocks ∈ [max(144, nr.delay_blocks), 35000]`, strictly below Namecoin mainnet's `36000`-block expiry so `B_1` cannot expire before its cancellation window closes).
2. **Three-step on-chain ceremony:**
   - **Step 1 (`T_offer` at `h_offer < h_13`):** Owner of `B_2` (`B_2.owner_policy`) broadcasts a confirmed owner-authored (`SIGHASH_ALL`) `OP_NAME_UPDATE` (`nVersion = 0x7100`) on `B_2.registration` creating a non-name native SegWit `consent_utxo = (T_offer, consent_vout)` locked strictly to `compile(B_2.owner_policy)`. `B_2` MUST remain un-expired, writer-verified (`SL-LDGR-007`), free of any `link.origin` (until Step 3 `h_act >= h_13 + D`), and invariant in `B_2.owner_policy` across the ENTIRE interval **`[h_offer, h_13 + D]`** (including `(h_offer, h_13)`; pre-offer key rotations strictly before `h_offer` are permitted and MUST NOT block Step 3 activation). Spending `consent_utxo` before `T_rebind` cancels the offer at UTXO consensus level.
   - **Step 2 (`T_rebind` at `h_13`):** Single PSBT (`nVersion = 0x7100`, Witness Profile `W`, matching inputs/outputs strictly by prevout & compiled script without hardcoded `vin`/`vout` indices) spending both `B_1`'s previous name UTXO (under current writer-verified `eff_pol_1 = effective_binding.owner_policy` immediately before `T_rebind`, allowing routine cold `owner_policy` rotation on `B_1` prior to `T_rebind` while verifying `B_1`'s policy at its anchor index — `offer_idx` when `B_1` itself entered via `binding_mode = "voluntary"`, or `updates[0]` for `0x0c` bindings — equals `active_binding.owner_policy`) and `consent_utxo` (under `B_2.owner_policy`), updating `B_1` to exact canonical `value = "{}"` while **preserving `compile(eff_pol_1)` unchanged** (so `0x10` cancellation and post-rebind `0x0b` compromise breaks remain executable on `B_1`), and carrying one zero-value owner-authored (`SIGHASH_ALL`) `SLNK 0x13 VOLUNTARY_REBIND` frame committing `object_id(B_2)`. A non-`SIGHASH_ALL` `0x13` frame (or a `0x13` confirmed while the origin is NOT yet autonomous `State 4`) is semantically inert and MUST NOT block admission. An owner-authored `0x13` on an autonomous origin whose preimage `B_2` is withheld or lost enters a bounded **unknown-preimage pending window** `[h_13, h_13 + min_d)` where `min_d = max(144, nr.delay_blocks)` under `dual_party_delayed` (or `144` under `mode = "none"`; if `min_d > 35000`, the unknown preimage cannot satisfy valid voluntary rebind delay bounds and is ignored): admission refuses `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` during `[h_13, h_13 + min_d)`, while a confirmed `0x10` (committing the frame's 32-byte commitment on `B_1`) OR a `dual_party_delayed` `0x0c` recovery strictly prior to `h_13 + min_d` cleanly rescues the origin. **Anti-decoy split-brain & stall rescue (`D14-B09`):** (a) if a **second** `0x13` frame arrives on `B_1` while `unknown_preimage` is already pending, the automaton MUST immediately enter **`unknown_rebind_stalled`** (failing closed `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE`, closing the decoy-`0x13` + `0x10` split-brain attack between verifiers with and without the decoy preimage); (b) once `H >= h_13 + min_d` without the preimage `B_2` (or upon a second `0x13` during `unknown_preimage`), the automaton enters **`unknown_rebind_stalled`** (failing closed `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` and rejecting late `0x10` cancels); (c) under `name_recovery.mode = "dual_party_delayed"`, a confirmed `0x0c` recovery (`disposer + arbiter`, with `supersedes_binding_id ∈ {id(B_1), stalled_cid_13}`) cleanly **rescues out of `unknown_rebind_stalled`** into `pending_recovery(B_3)` so a stolen `B_1` key emitting a garbage `0x13 <random32>` can NEVER permanently kill-switch the origin (both verifiers with and without the preimage converge deterministically to `B_3`!). Malformed `SLNK` frames on `B_1` are scoped strictly forward-only from `h_frame` and never break already-promoted `B_2` or historical `at_height < h_frame`. Any invalid presented `0x13` preimage or failed precondition is deterministically rejected without setting `recovery_burned`. **Active-log operational precondition:** an actor operating an active `0x14` transparency/denial log (`SPEC-05 §6`) MUST terminate (`terminal = true`) that log on `B_1` before executing `0x13`, because `log_id` binds `issuer_name` and `issuer_registration`.
   - **Delay window `[h_13, h_13 + D)` & two cancellation channels:** During `[h_13, h_13 + D)`, both `B_1` and `B_2` refuse **`RECOVERY_FORBIDDEN`**. Strictly prior to `h_13 + D` (inclusive of `H == h_13`): (a) under `dual_party_delayed`, a valid `0x0c` (`disposer + arbiter`, `supersedes_binding_id = id(B_1)`) intercepts `0x13` cleanly without `recovery_burned` and enters `pending_recovery`; (b) under all modes, an owner-authored (`SIGHASH_ALL`) `0x10` (`id(B_2)`) on `B_1` (under `eff_pol_1`) OR `B_2` (under `B_2.owner_policy`) immediately aborts `0x13` and restores `B_1`.
   - **Step 3 (`awaiting_activation` gate at `h_act >= h_13 + D`) & tri-state `B_2` invariance:** Upon maturing at `H == h_13 + D`, the verifier evaluates `B_2`'s `[h_offer, h_13 + D]` invariance as a **three-valued** status (`valid | invalid | incomplete`): if `B_2`'s history is truncated or unavailable (`incomplete`), the verifier MUST fail closed **`ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE`** (NEVER silently reverting to `B_1`!); if conclusively violated (`invalid`), the rebind aborts cleanly back to `B_1`; if `valid`, `B_2` supersedes `B_1` (`B_1` permanently refuses `ORIGIN_BINDING_INVALID` for live admission) and enters **`awaiting_activation`** until `B_2.owner_policy` confirms an owner-authored (`SIGHASH_ALL`) `OP_NAME_UPDATE` at `h_act >= h_13 + D` writing `link.origin = origin_id` (and continuing writer-verified / non-expired through `eval_h`, updating `effective_binding.owner_policy` to the current writer-verified policy on `B_2`). While in `awaiting_activation`, live admission refuses `ORIGIN_BINDING_INVALID` and a second `0x13` (`B_2 -> B_3`) is forbidden, while emergency `0x0c` recovery remains permitted.
3. **Deterministic 5-subphase per-height order (`1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 -> 4b`, zero `tx_index` dependence) & fail-closed automaton budget:** Verifiers schedule ONLY relevant state-transition heights (status/emancipation, `0x0c`/`0x13` proposals and deadlines, `0x10` cancels, and Step-3 activation — NEVER ordinary ward renewals, routine updates, or discarded/malformed frames; post-loop termination checks are decoupled from the step budget), enforce a fail-closed upper bound of `MAX_AUTOMATON_STEPS = 1024` (refusing **`ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE`** — NEVER returning partial active state — if exceeded), and apply `max_history_steps` consistently across every lineage walk. For every ascending event height `H` in `[h_bind, eval_h]`:
   - **Phase 1:** Apply `status_seal` / emancipation handover (including delayed `H_self > H_promo` self-update emancipation for post-trigger `B_rec`) and Step 3 `B_2` activation.
   - **Phase 2:** Mature pending `0x0c` (`H == h_rec + D_rec`, resetting any superseded `emancipated_owner_policy` on post-emancipation `0x0c` recovery and tracking `effective_binding.owner_policy` on autonomous recoveries) and pending `0x13` (`H == h_13 + D_13`: fail closed `ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE` if `B_2` is `incomplete`, abort back to `B_1` if `B_2` is `invalid`, or promote to `awaiting_activation` if `valid`; transition `unknown_preimage` at `H == h_13 + min_d` into `unknown_rebind_stalled`).
   - **Phase 3:** Apply `0x0c` intercept and `0x10` cancellations on previously opened pending transitions (`H < deadline`; an already-emancipated `State 4` origin MAY cancel a subsequent `0x0c` via `0x10` even after calendar majority `T` or `capacity/1`).
   - **Phase 4:** Apply new proposals, processing `0x0c` **strictly before** `0x13` (so same-block `0x0c` beats `0x13` without `recovery_burned`).
   - **Phase 4b:** Process same-block `0x10` cancellations confirmed at `H == h_rec` or `H == h_13` against transitions opened in Phase 4, enforcing exact half-open windows `[h_start, h_start + D)`.

## 7. Security Considerations

- **Three-place key custody limit:** A `2-of-3` child name (`Parent A`, `Parent B`, `Notary`) survives any 1 lost share, but any 2 colluding holders can spend the name UTXO. Preventing both single-point loss and 2-party collusion requires `3-of-4` with an independent 4th custodian.
- **Name renewal obligation:** Over an 18-year childhood on Namecoin (`D = 36000`), guardians execute ~27–30 renewals. Off-chain agreements SHOULD define when the 3rd custodian co-signs renewals while keeping the on-chain script at full threshold (`SL-LDGR-006`).
- **Compromised issuer key:** Independent `arbiter_pk` requirements (`SL-ORIG-007`, `SL-ORIG-013`, `SL-ORIG-015`) prevent unilateral appointment, roster takeover, or name recovery; institutional authorities SHOULD configure `BranchKeySpec` native `m-of-n` (`2 <= n <= 5`) or qualified threshold ECDSA for `disposer_pk`, `arbiter_pk`, `challenger_pk`, and cold multisig `owner_policy`.
- **Institutional DoS boundary on `status_seal`:** Because a non-`0x09` or malformed spend of `status_seal` transitions the status spine to terminal `incomplete`, a malicious or misconfigured holder of the `status_seal` key (`capacity_authority` / `end_authority`) can cause an institutional denial of service that fails safely closed (`ORIGIN_STATUS_INCOMPLETE`), halting future admission for that origin without enabling forged emancipation or forged subject end. Conversely, a malformed spend of `recovery_seal` burns only future recovery (`recovery_burned`) without killing the active binding `B_k`. Institutional authorities SHOULD guard `status_seal` keys with cold native `P2WSH` multisig or qualified threshold ECDSA custody.
- **Corporate governance recipe in v1.0:** Because `organization` origins omit `guardian_policy`, corporate governance composes cleanly from v1.0 core primitives: cold `P2WSH` `m-of-n` `owner_policy` (shareholders/founders), `link.grant = {"p": sha256(m-of-n)}` (board/executive quorum), depth-34 salted `sets.members` (`seallink-member-entry/1` with `roles[]` and disposable revocation seals), and bounded `seallink-grant/1` delegations to officers and agents.
- **Reversible adult capacity extension path:** SealLink v1.0 enforces strictly one-way `ward -> autonomous` capacity. Reversible adult conservatorship (e.g., temporary judicial guardianship and subsequent restoration) can be introduced in a future Optional Module SLIP extending the `status_seal` spine and critical forward-only `0x15`–`0x1a` actions (`SPEC-02 §5.2`) without altering v1.0 wire formats or breaking historical verifications.
