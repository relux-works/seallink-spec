## [1.0.0-draft.8] - 2026-11-24

- **Decision `1B` — Unified Emancipation & Handover + Historical Ward Contract Verification (`SPEC-04`, `SPEC-05`, `R1 #1`, `R2G-04`):**
  - Historical `at_height`/`at_time` evaluation of ward origin status, `0x0f` roster revisions, guardian slot lifecycles, and guardian `link.grant` keys (with policy-scoped retroactive `0x0b` compromise checks) so already-anchored ward contracts (`0x07`) survive later roster transitions (`0x0f`) and routine guardian key rotations.
  - Unified emancipation state machine across `person` and `agent`: calendar majority `T` (`person` only) triggers `State 1 -> State 3 (GUARDIAN_TRANSFER_PENDING)` and immediately revokes former-guardian `0x10`; entering `State 4 (autonomous)` ALWAYS requires either confirmed `seallink-capacity/1` + handover + adult/subject `SIGHASH_ALL` self-update OR confirmed `0x0c` dual-party recovery (`seq >= 2`).
- **Decision `2A` — Full Hardening of Optional-Declaration v1.0 Modules (`CONFORMANCE.md §1.1`, `SPEC-06 §5`, `SPEC-07 §2–§3`):**

- **Decision `3` — Journal Capacity, Signed-Receipt Reconciliation & Unified 1-Entry Single-Use (`SPEC-01`, `SPEC-06`, `R1 #2/#3`, `R3 B2`):**
  - Raised `max_bytes` ceilings (`65536` for `seallink-journal-entry/1` & `seallink-manifest/1`; `1048576` for `seallink-members-list/1`) and enforced Draft 2020-12 `JournalEntry` schema validation on `FencedJournal.append()`.

- **Decision `4` — Canonical `ScriptPolicy` Pair Ordering & Strict Key Disjointness (`SPEC-02`, `schemas/common.schema.json`, `SL-LEDG-011`):**
  - Enforced canonical lexicographic ordering (`a < b`) on `both_of_2`, `either_of_2`, and `arbiter_or_both`, plus pairwise x-coordinate disjointness across all branch roles (`SL-LEDG-011`, bringing total unique rules to `114`).

- **Decision `5` — Immutable Multi-Release Web Archive & Strict Tag Publishing (`tools/build_site.py`, `.github/workflows/deploy-site.yml`):**
