## [1.0.0-draft.5] - 2026-11-23

commitments, explicit `disposer_pk`/`arbiter_pk`/`challenger_pk`, authenticated `status_seal` / `roster_seal` / `recovery_seal` continuation spines (including `status_seal` institutional DoS boundary and forbidden `retain_active`/`retain_vacant` on `disposer_pk`/`arbiter_pk` rotation), direct initial (`0x12`) and acyclic two-stage (`0x0d`/`0x0e`) guardianship with mandatory incoming guardian
